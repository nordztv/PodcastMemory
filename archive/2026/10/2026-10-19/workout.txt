MORNING BRIEFING WORKOUT - Monday 19 October 2026
Focus: Full body
Equipment: 2 x 7.5 kg dumbbells, bodyweight, a bit of floor space
Total time: about 25 minutes

WARM-UP (4 minutes)
March on the spot, 1 minute, swinging your arms
Arm circles, 30 seconds each direction
10 slow bodyweight squats
10 hip hinges (hands on hips, push your hips back, flat back)
5 inchworms (walk your hands out to a plank and back)

MAIN WORKOUT (3 rounds, 40 seconds work / 20 seconds rest per exercise, 90 seconds rest between rounds)
1. Goblet squat - hold one dumbbell at your chest. Sit back and down, chest tall, knees track over your toes.
2. Bent-over row - both dumbbells. Hinge forward with a flat back, pull elbows towards your hips, squeeze your shoulder blades.
3. Reverse lunge - dumbbells at your sides, alternate legs. Step back softly, front knee stays over the ankle.
4. Floor press - lie on your back, knees bent. Lower until your elbows touch the floor softly, then press up.
5. Romanian deadlift - dumbbells in front of your thighs. Push your hips back, slight knee bend, feel the hamstrings, stand tall.
6. Plank - forearms down, straight line from head to heels, squeeze your glutes. Knees down is fine.

FINISHER (optional, 3 minutes)
Alternate 30 seconds of dumbbell thrusters (squat into overhead press) with 30 seconds of fast step-backs (stepping back and forward, no jumping), 3 times.

COOL-DOWN (3 minutes)
Standing quad stretch, 30 seconds each side
Hamstring stretch (heel forward, hinge), 30 seconds each side
Child's pose, 1 minute

Great way to start the week, Scott. Every session adds up.
