MORNING BRIEFING WORKOUT - Sunday 18 October 2026
Focus: Upper body circuit
Equipment: 2 x 7.5 kg dumbbells, bodyweight
Total time: about 15 minutes

WARM-UP (3 minutes)
Arm circles, 30 seconds each direction
Shoulder rolls and arm swings across the chest, 1 minute
10 slow bodyweight squats

MAIN WORKOUT (3 rounds, 1 minute rest between rounds)
1. Floor press - 10 reps. Lie on your back, elbows touch the floor softly, press straight up.
2. Bent-over row - 10 reps each arm. Flat back, pull the elbow toward your hip.
3. Standing overhead press - 10 reps. Squeeze your glutes and brace your stomach, don't lean back.
4. Push-ups - as many as you can with good form. Knees down is fine; keep a straight line from head to knees.

COOL-DOWN (2 minutes)
Chest stretch in a doorway, 30 seconds each side
Cross-body shoulder stretch, 30 seconds each side
