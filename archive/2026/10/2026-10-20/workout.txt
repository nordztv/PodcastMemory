MORNING BRIEFING WORKOUT - Tuesday 20 October 2026
Focus: Upper body pull and core
Equipment: 2 x 7.5 kg dumbbells, bodyweight, a bit of floor space
Total time: about 25 minutes

WARM-UP (4 minutes)
March on the spot, 1 minute, swinging your arms
Arm circles, 30 seconds each direction
Shoulder blade squeezes, 15 slow reps (pull your shoulders back and down, hold for a second)
10 cat-cows on hands and knees
10 hip hinges (hands on hips, push your hips back, flat back)

MAIN WORKOUT (3 rounds, 40 seconds work / 20 seconds rest per exercise, 90 seconds rest between rounds)
1. Bent-over row - both dumbbells. Hinge forward with a flat back, pull your elbows towards your hips, squeeze your shoulder blades at the top.
2. Single-arm row - one hand and knee on a sturdy chair or couch, row one dumbbell towards your hip. 20 seconds each side. Keep your back flat and don't twist.
3. Reverse fly - hinge forward, slight bend in the elbows, lift the dumbbells out to the sides like wings. Go slow and light; lead with the elbows.
4. Dumbbell pullover - lie on your back, knees bent, hold one dumbbell with both hands above your chest. Lower it slowly behind your head, keep your ribs down, bring it back up.
5. Dead bug - lie on your back, arms up, knees at 90 degrees. Lower the opposite arm and leg slowly, then switch. Keep your lower back pressed into the floor.
6. Side plank - on your forearm, knees down or legs straight. 20 seconds each side. Keep a straight line from head to knees or feet.

FINISHER (optional, 3 minutes)
Alternate 30 seconds of renegade rows (in a plank on your knees or toes, row one dumbbell at a time) with 30 seconds of slow mountain climbers (step, don't jump), 3 times.

COOL-DOWN (3 minutes)
Child's pose, 1 minute
Doorway chest stretch, 30 seconds each side
Thread the needle (on hands and knees, reach one arm under your body), 30 seconds each side

A strong back makes everything else easier. Lovely work today, Scott.
