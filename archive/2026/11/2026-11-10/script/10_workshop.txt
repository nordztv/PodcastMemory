[happy] Workshop time! Tonight's build step: the first frame of Lanternfall. About forty five minutes, and you'll press play on your own game.

[calm] Step one. Open your Lanternfall project in Godot. In the FileSystem panel at the bottom left, right click on res, choose Create New, then Folder, and make four folders: scenes, scripts, art and audio.

[calm] Step two, the pixel settings, from the game tech segment. Open Project, then Project Settings. Turn on Advanced Settings with the switch at the top right, so everything shows. Then: Rendering, Textures, Default Texture Filter: Nearest. Display, Window: Viewport Width three twenty, Height one eighty, Window Width Override twelve eighty, Height Override seven twenty. And under Stretch: Mode viewport, Scale Mode integer. {Close|Kloze} the window. It saves by itself.

[calm] Step three, the Main scene. Click Scene, then New Scene, and choose Other Node. Pick a plain Node two D, and rename it Main. Save it into your scenes folder as main dot T S C N.

[calm] Step four, the ground. Click the plus button to add a child node to Main, and add a Color Rect. In the Inspector, set its colour to a soft grass green, and under Layout, Transform, set its Size to three twenty by one eighty, so it fills the whole screen. That's your farm, for now. [teasing] It's a very minimalist farm. [punch] Very premium. Very Scandinavian. [giggling while talking] Okay, back to work.

[calm] Step five, a stand-in farmer. Add another child to Main: a Sprite two D. Drag the icon dot S V G file from the FileSystem onto its Texture slot in the Inspector.
