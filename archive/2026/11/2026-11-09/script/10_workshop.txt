[calm] Step one: the monster scene. Make a new scene with a Character Body two D as the root, and name it Radish Monster. [calm] Step two: the burst. Attach a script to Radish Monster. In its ready function, set scale to Vector two dot zero, written in capitals, so it starts invisible. Then make a tween with create tween, and tween the property scale, to Vector two dot one, also in capitals, over zero point two seconds. Then chain on set trans, with Tween dot trans back, and set ease, with Tween dot ease out. That's the spring-out-of-the-ground pop. Run the scene on its own with F six, and it should pop up in the middle of the screen.

[calm] Step three: the alarm. Open your Game State autoload. Near the top, add a signal: signal radish awakened, and in brackets, where, colon, Vector two. [calm] Step four: pass the position. In your radish dot G D script, wherever the radish gets pulled, call Game State dot register pull, and pass in global position. Now every pull tells Game State exactly where it happened.

[calm] Step five: the spawner. Open your Farm scene's script. At the top, add the cookie cutter: const Radish Monster Scene equals preload, and the path to radish monster dot T S C N in quotes.
