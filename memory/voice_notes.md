# Voice notes from her people

**The feature (Scott's feature 2, 12 Nov 2026).** Now and then Mizuki plays Scott a "voice note" from someone in her life. It is really her retelling a message she got, in their words and their way of talking. Examples:
- "Mum says, quote: you're late, and also, are you eating."
- Kenta's two-word texts.
- Mr Kondo's single line with a fish emoji.
- Lily's voice memo that is four minutes long.

**How often:** about two a week, never the same person in two days of calls running.

**Where the messages come from:**
- the people dice ("news from someone in her life"),
- her arcs,
- real news (her dad after a Tigers win, Aya after an FFXIV patch),
- or just everyday life: a photo of Hana, Chiyo's soroban.

**How to write them:**
- Each person sounds like their people page in `show/mizuki/people/`: their catchphrases, how long their messages are, their language. Japanese family write in Japanese, and she translates as she goes.
- No one sends Scott messages directly, and no one comments on him beyond a light "who is this Scott?" from her mum. Nothing romantic.
- Log each one here, so the same message isn't used twice and the people's stories stay consistent.
