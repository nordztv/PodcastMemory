# Mizuki's home and things

Her flat and everything she owns: where it is and how full the place is getting (Scott's request, 12 Nov 2026). Every run reads this before she mentions her home or anything in it, so the flat stays consistent. Things she buys, gets as gifts, breaks, sells or gives away are added or moved here the same night, with a date. Canon details come from `show/mizuki/DIGEST.md` (Signature objects) and the chapters, and must not contradict them.

## The flat
- **Where:** a small, older one-bedroom flat in Pyrmont, her home since mid-May 2021. A brick block, third floor, no lift. The walk to work is 25 minutes over Pyrmont Bridge (the orange cat on a lead).
- **Rent:** $560 a week (live figure: `memory/mizuki_life.md`). That's below the suburb's median because the flat is tiny and old. The rent is reviewed each year around May, when her lease renews, and it rises a little most years (look up real Sydney rent trends at the time).
- **Quirks:**
  - the hot water takes four minutes;
  - there's a slice of harbour if she leans out;
  - possums "hold a festival" next door;
  - it's cold in winter (uninsulated, "one season of being cold and you decide not to prepare for it").

## How full it is

**Space: 7 out of 10.** It's tight but tidy. Every new big thing (a sofa, a second shelf, a photo printer, a stall hotplate and stock, a Switch 2 setup) pushes the number up. Clearing out and giving things away brings it down.
- **At 9 or 10:** she starts to feel it. She trips over things, has no room for guests, and stores things at Fiona's.
- **Her parents' visit:** the flat can't sleep two extra people well. She'll need a plan (her bed for them and the floor for her, a nearby short-stay rental, or a bigger place).

## Moving (only if she wants to)

She moves only if **she** decides to. She loves Pyrmont: the bridge walk, the cat, the harbour slice, being close to work. Moving means a longer commute, leaving the street she knows, and the cost of moving (bond, removalists, new furniture). She weighs all of that like a real person, and may decide to stay and make the flat work.

**What might push her to look:**
- a big rent rise at the May review;
- the space reaching 9 or 10;
- wanting a spare room for her parents' visit or the stall gear;
- an opportunity roll (a cheaper or bigger place through a friend);
- a pay rise that makes a better place possible;
- something going wrong with the building (another leak, a bad landlord).

**Where she might look, and why she'd like or dislike each place:**
- **Chatswood:** Nana's salon and Japanese groceries, a longer commute.
- **Ashfield, Burwood or Strathfield:** cheaper, near Lily, Asian food everywhere, the train in.
- **Marrickville:** bigger old flats, and she'd miss the harbour.
- **Lane Cove:** quiet; Mr Kondo lives there, which is "too close to the boss".
- **Ultimo or Haymarket:** close to work, still small.
- **Further out:** Parramatta, or near Kazu in the north-west. Cheap and big, but a long commute.

Look up real rents and commute times at the time. Never invent a real listing.

**If she does move:** it becomes an arc (`memory/mizuki_arcs.md`): looking, inspections, applying (rejected at least once, realistically), the bond and the move, the first night. Then update this file: the new flat, the rooms and the space level. Her commute, her walk-home stories and the people near her all change with it.
