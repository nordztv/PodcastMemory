# Mizuki's ledger

Every dollar into or out of her savings funds, one dated line each, written by `python3 engine/ledger.py move DATE AMOUNT "Fund" "what for"` (it updates the funds table in `memory/mizuki_life.md` at the same time, and `python3 engine/ledger.py check` proves the funds still add up). Everyday spending inside her weekly budget isn't listed. Started 13 Nov 2026 with $20,000 across seven funds. Never edit old lines; correct a mistake with a new line.
