# Mizuki's life: money, goals and weekly journal

Mizuki's simulated life (Scott's request, 11 Nov 2026): her wage, budget, savings, goals and a weekly journal, kept realistic for a hospitality worker renting in inner Sydney. Every daily run reads it so what she says on air fits her money and plans. The **Sunday run** (the one making Monday's episode) updates it (`show/daily_run.md` step 2b). Prices are realistic Sydney prices in Australian dollars; when she buys or books something specific, look up the real current price first. Everything here is fiction about a fictional character. Scott can edit anything.

## Income

- **Job:** shift lead at the ramen shop at World Square (Mr Kondo's), full time since 2021 (joined early 2019). Usually 10:30am to 7pm, Monday to Friday, about 38 to 40 hours a week.
- **Pay:** $38.00 an hour (Scott's choice, 11 Nov 2026; above the Restaurant Industry Award, where Level 4 is $29.45 from 1 July 2026, because Mr Kondo pays her more as his shift lead). About $1,445 to $1,520 a week before tax, about $77,000 a year.
- **Take-home:** about $1,190 a week after income tax and the Medicare levy (2026-27 rates, rounded). Plus about $20 a week in tips from the shared jar. Employer superannuation (12%) goes to her super fund, not her pocket.
- **Extras now and then:** overtime on busy weeks (+$100 to $250), a small Christmas bonus from Mr Kondo (about $300), selling the odd print (see goals).

## Goals

Big goals take months or years; modest ones days or weeks. Each has a status and a dated progress line. When one is achieved, she's genuinely happy on air (and it goes in the journal). New goals come from her history and what's happening in her life; retire goals that no longer fit, with a reason.

### Big

1. **Bring her parents to Sydney for the first time** (target $7,000; hoped for September 2027, when the shop can close for a fortnight; her father has never been on a long flight). Status: saving.
2. **Okonomiyaki pop-up stall** at a Sydney weekend market (target $15,000 over two to three years: a stall fee, a big hotplate, permits and a food safety course, a gazebo, first stock). First step: a single trial day at a market by late 2027. Status: saving, researching.
3. **A small photo exhibition** of her Sydney photos, "Barefoot Country", in a café gallery (prints and framing about $1,200, by late 2027). Status: choosing photos.
4. **Conversational Korean and a trip to Seoul for a TWICE concert** (about $3,500, 2028). Status: Duolingo streak, Lily as tutor.
