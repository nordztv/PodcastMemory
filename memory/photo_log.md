# Mizuki's photo of the week

**Every Friday (Scott's feature 3, 12 Nov 2026),** she describes one photo she took that week, the way a photographer would:
- what it shows, and where and when she took it;
- the camera (the Zf with its 40mm, or the FM2 on film);
- the light, why she framed it that way, and what she'd do differently;
- a small tip for Scott's ZR when it fits.

The photo comes from her real week:
- her walk over Pyrmont Bridge,
- the shop,
- her weekend plans,
- the real Sydney weather and light that day,
- or a dice event (a ruined roll, a lucky shot).

**Photo of the Month:** on the last Friday of each month, she picks her favourite of the month's photos. It goes on her timeline page as a card: title, description, camera and settings.

**Film:** one roll a month on the FM2 is one of her modest goals. When she develops a roll (at a real Sydney lab, at a real price), she talks about the whole roll.
