# October 2026: monthly summary

The show's first month. A sample episode on 18 Oct, then daily weekday episodes from 19 Oct (10 weekday episodes, 19-30 Oct).

## Main stories and how they developed
- **Israel's election:** campaign and the IDF-Netanyahu feud, vote on 27 Oct, exit polls, then a hung count: Likud 26 the largest party, Netanyahu's bloc 53 v Eisenkot's 48, Arab-led parties a record 19 as kingmakers; Likud's "stolen" claim; formal results 4 Nov.
- **Iran war and oil:** Saudi refinery strike, missile intercepts, Brent around US$115 by month's end; Iran defiant through Araghchi.
- **Brazil:** Lula re-elected 51.8-48.2; Flavio Bolsonaro rejected the result, then his party accepted it.
- **Spain:** Urda high-speed crash (68+ dead) on 28 Oct, a safety-system fault and an "inexplicable" restart order.
- **US:** Fed held 10-2 (Cook and Hammack wanted a hike); midterms set for 3 Nov.
- **Australia:** inflation (CPI 4.3%) pushed RBA hike odds above 80% for 3 Nov; Albanese promising MYEFO savings; Marrickville golf grant row; MP security and the digital duty of care bill (through the House); Coalition rejected the EU FTA, Greens open to it; SAS culture report released; Senate estimates (Snowy 2.0 $22-25b, aged care algorithm, ATO card ban); working holiday visa cuts.
- **NSW and Sydney:** Southwest Metro opened 30 Oct; Star fined $260m; $6.8b South Coast rail; gun buyback announced for 2 Nov; Road Safety Plan 2031; WSI opening.
- **Tech:** memory supercycle: SK Hynix and Samsung record quarters (Hynix 80% margin, HBM sold out to 2029), Kospi past 6,000; GPU prices rising with memory; Intel Arc Pro B70 32GB (prices climbing); Apple's M6 iMac, M6 MacBook Pro and touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro (stores 6 Nov), OLED iPad mini; Anthropic's S-1; Starship Flight 15 prep; Fujifilm X-T6 rumours.
- **Games:** WoW Forever (name reservations, launch 5 Nov AEDT); FFXIV Fan Fest build-up (Evercold) and the FF7R Keybound Brawler backlash; Phantom Blade Zero's big launch; Ocarina of Time remake (5 Nov); Virtua Fighter Crossroads by RGG; Witcher at 19; retro projects (Panzer Dragoon Saga PC, Blast Corps recomp, Ogaerito translation).
- **Movies:** Godzilla Minus Zero (R-rated, Japan 3 Nov, AU 5 Nov); Tokyo IFF; Korean box office (Chiikawa, Paradise Lost); Clayface; Dune Part Three trailers; Fangoria Chainsaw Awards; lots of 4K news (The Host, Train to Busan, I Saw the Devil, Chinese Ghost Story).
