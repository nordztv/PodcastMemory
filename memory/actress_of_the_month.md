# Actress of the Month

Tuesdays: a film of hers to watch. Thursdays: how that film was made, or a retrospective. An actress each month from cult horror, sci-fi, fantasy, crime, Japanese or Korean cinema. Actresses can return in later months (ideally to build director connections), but a film is never covered twice. Newest first. Scott can suggest someone by adding a line here or telling any Claude chat.
