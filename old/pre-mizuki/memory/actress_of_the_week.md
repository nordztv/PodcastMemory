# Actress of the Week

Mizuki's obsession of the week (Scott's choice, 11 Nov 2026, replacing Actress of the Month): each week she watches a film and gets a little obsessed with its actress, and talks about her all week in the movies part (the film on Monday or Tuesday, then her best scenes, other films, a making-of story, a fact she found at 2am). A new actress each week, cult and genre leaning, Japanese and Korean first, connected to directors and films already featured where possible. Never the same film twice (`memory/movie_log.md`). Celebrate the performance and style; nothing about real people's bodies. Newest first. Scott can suggest someone here.
