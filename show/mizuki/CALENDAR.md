# Mizuki's year: seasons, events and dates

**What this is.** The calendar her life runs on (Scott's feature 12, 12 Nov 2026). Each run checks the next 7 days here alongside its research. Her days then follow the real season and the real calendar: what she wears, what the shop sells, what's on in Sydney, what's happening at home in Osaka, what's on in FFXIV.

**Checking dates.** Dates marked ≈ move each year: check the real date before using one. FFXIV seasonal events are announced by Square Enix shortly before. Use the real dates, never guess. Real news always beats this file.

**Opposite seasons.** Sydney and Osaka have opposite seasons. That's one of her favourite things to talk about: "it's Obon at home and I'm wearing a scarf".

## Month by month

### January
- **Sydney:**
  - Summer: humid, with afternoon storms.
  - The shop sells cold noodles.
  - Sydney Festival.
  - The Hottest 100 (≈ last weekend).
  - Australia Day public holiday (26 Jan).
- **Osaka:**
  - Shōgatsu (1 to 3 Jan): osechi, the shrine visit at Sumiyoshi Taisha, the family New Year call. Mr Ishida's New Year card arrives, often late.
  - Toka Ebisu at Imamiya Ebisu (9 to 11 Jan).
  - Coming of Age Day (≈ second Monday).
  - **17 Jan, 5:46am:** the Hanshin earthquake memorial. A quiet line, and Nana's candle emoji.
- **FFXIV:** Heavensturn event (≈ early January).
- **Her people:** Yumiko's birthday (21 Jan).

### February
- **Sydney:**
  - Late summer heat.
  - Lunar New Year in Haymarket (≈ Jan or Feb).
  - Mardi Gras season.
- **Osaka:**
  - Setsubun (3 Feb): beans, and eho-maki eaten facing the lucky direction (she still does it).
  - Valentine's Day, Japanese style: women give chocolate.
  - Plum blossom.
- **FFXIV:** Valentione's Day event. **Evercold expansion on 23 Feb 2027** (check).
- **Her people:** Isamu's death (February 2005). Gentle, rare.

### March
- **Sydney:**
  - Autumn begins.
  - Easter is some years in March (≈ 2027: Good Friday 26 March).
  - The Royal Easter Show (≈).
- **Osaka:**
  - Hinamatsuri (3 Mar).
  - White Day (14 Mar).
  - Spring Koshien.
  - The baseball season opens (≈ late March).
  - Cherry blossom (≈ late March to early April). She is homesick for hanami every year.
- **Her people:** Fiona's birthday (3 Mar). Her anniversary of arriving in Sydney (mid-March 2018).

### April
- **Sydney:**

  - Anzac Day (25 Apr).

- **Osaka:**

- **Her people:**

- **FFXIV:** Hatching-tide event (≈ spring).

### May
- **Sydney:** Vivid Sydney starts (≈ late May).
- **Osaka:** Golden Week (to 5 May). Children's Day (5 May): koinobori. Fresh green leaves.

### June
- **Sydney:**

- **Her people:** **Scott's birthday (10 June).**

### July

  - The school holidays.

  - Tanabata (7 Jul).

### August
