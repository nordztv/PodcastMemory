# Mizuki: canon spine

The master list of fixed facts for Mizuki's life story. Every chapter in `show/mizuki/history/` and every page in `show/mizuki/people/` must agree with this file. Where the older single-file story (now only in the repository's history) is more detailed and doesn't conflict, its details stand. If anything conflicts, this spine wins. Written 11 Nov 2026 for the expanded story (Scott's request); updated to agree with `RESOLUTIONS.md` and the fifteen chapters.

## People (core facts)

**Family (Tanaka, Abeno ward, Osaka)**
- **Mizuki Tanaka** — born Tuesday 10 April 1990, Abeno, Osaka. 158 cm. Right-handed. Blood type A (her mother says this explains everything). Hufflepuff. Favourite Pokémon Vulpix (Rokon).
- **Hiroshi Tanaka** (father) — born 3 August 1958, Abeno; Isamu and Chiyo's only child. Second-generation okonomiyaki cook: full-time in the shop from about 1977, proper wages from 1982, Isamu's partner from his marriage in 1987, the hotplate fully his from about 1994. Dry, deadpan boke humour; says little; "This is correct" is his highest food praise, "kakkoii" his highest praise of anything else. Hanshin Tigers devotee ("mada mada"). Bought his Nikon FM2 in 1982 with his first proper wages; gave it to Mizuki in 2006. Loves Southern All Stars and old enka, fishing off the rocks in Wakayama on rare days off. Bad back since a slipped disc in May 2023 (shop closed two weeks). Calls natto "Tokyo glue".
- **Yumiko Tanaka**, née Morita (mother) — born 21 January 1961, Abeno, Fumiko's only daughter; formerly on the Kintetsu department store gift counter. Runs the front, the till, the regulars and the family (daily figures from the early 1990s). Sharp tsukkomi; hits Hiroshi with the menu. Takarazuka Revue fan (promised Mizuki a show the night of the July 1999 bike fall; first show at the Grand Theatre in October 1999; Mizuki took her again for Mizuki's 20th in 2010). Karaoke queen. Says "you're late" instead of "welcome home". Married Hiroshi on 8 November 1987.
- **Kenta Tanaka** (brother) — born 22 November 1993. Was Dragon Quest to her Final Fantasy. Studied information engineering (from April 2012); systems engineer at an IT company in Osaka from April 2016; own flat from spring 2017 (Tennoji ward, ten minutes from the shop). Engaged New Year 2021; married **Miho** (née Sato, born 1995, a nursery teacher from Sakai) in late October 2021. Daughter **Hana**, born 14 April 2024 ("my small boss"; Mizuki first held her in June 2025). Quiet, practical, sends photos without words; sent her a little microphone for video calls in the 2020 lockdown.
- **Fumiko Morita** (maternal grandmother) — 1927 to early December 2016 (died peacefully in hospital aged 89 after a winter chest cold, Mizuki holding her hand). Widow from 1988. Lived around the corner in an old wooden two-room house (morning glories, family altar, kotatsu, smell of incense and mothballs). Yokai storyteller; sang "Yūyake Koyake" to Mizuki under the kotatsu during the 1995 earthquake; Saturday trips to Kuromon market; taught her origami and how to fold a crane in the dark. Beiju party 2015. Left Mizuki her tin of origami paper.
- **Shigeru Morita** (maternal grandfather) — 1924 to 1988, died aged 64. A tram driver on the Hankai line. Mizuki never met him; his photo and cap are by the altar; Fumiko said he whistled two notes at every stop (Mizuki still does).
- **Isamu Tanaka** (paternal grandfather) — 1932 to February 2005. Opened the okonomiyaki shop in Abeno in October 1964, the year of the Tokyo Olympics. Loud, generous, Nankai Hawks fan until 1988, then Tigers. After handing over the hotplate he came in every morning at ten (not Wednesdays) to sit on seat one. Died suddenly in February 2005, in Mizuki's second year of junior high (she was 14). Left her his Seiko: steel case and steel bracelet, cream face, bought in 1964 to mark the opening, strap swapped once, hand-wound every night. She still wears it (her "old man's watch").
- **Chiyo Tanaka** (paternal grandmother) — born 1935, from Kishiwada. "The Minister of Finance": kept the shop's books in pencil for thirty years, handed the daily figures to Yumiko in the early 1990s, then checked the takings every Sunday afternoon. She and Isamu lived three streets away near the Hankai tram line, in a house with bonsai; she stayed there alone after he died. A fall in 2022, then a care home in Abeno (2022); a little forgetful, still sharp about money. Bought the strawberry-print Mezzo Piano dress. Mizuki visits on every trip home.
- **Daisuke Tanaka** (second cousin, "cousin") — born July 1985, Kobe, son of Hiroshi's cousin **Makoto** (shipping company, Kobe port) and **Keiko**. Elementary from 1992, junior high 1998, high school 2001. Brought the Super Famicom to Fumiko's after the 1995 earthquake and left it there; gave it to Mizuki in 2017 (now boxed in Kenta's old room; the Super Famicom Mini went to Sydney). Played FFVII in 1997 with Mizuki watching; calls her "the audience". Accountant at a Sannomiya firm from 2008; married with two sons.

Tsukkomi to Mizuki's boke ("rice and pickles"). Studied pharmacy at a different university in Kyoto; qualified 2015; now a hospital pharmacist in Osaka. Single, with a shiba inu called Mame. FFXIV Paladin since August 2013 (talked Mizuki into it; Mizuki raised her in Toto-Rak). Kanjani Eight fan. Togepi is her Pokémon. Gryffindor. Older sister Kana.

Mizuki's boss May 2009 to March 2013. Hawks fan. Sends brush-written New Year cards. His bowl is the only ten in her ramen notebook.

Father works for Kyoto city; mother teaches tea ceremony; he lived with them until Tokyo. Mizuki's boyfriend from 16 August 2012 to April 2017. Calm, kind, a bit boring about anime ("a bit complicated"). From April 2013 at a Kyoto precision-parts company. Taught her "talk to them, then stop talking" (Mizuki says it was her father's rule too; both said it) and "One more".

## Shinto and customs

Added 11 Nov 2026 at Scott's request. Keep it light, warm and specific; never preachy.
- **Stance:** like most Japanese families, Shinto and Buddhism side by side, "Shinto for life, Buddhism for death". The Buddhist family altars (Fumiko's, with Shigeru; the Tanaka one upstairs above the shop) stay as they are. Mizuki: "I'm not religious. I'm just Japanese." She keeps the customs because they are family, the seasons and her grandmothers.
- **The shop's kamidana:** put up by Isamu, high on the wall behind the hotplate, when the shop opened in October 1964; holds an ofuda from Sumiyoshi Taisha, renewed every New Year (the old one goes back to be burned). Every morning before opening Hiroshi puts out water, salt and rice, then bows twice, claps twice, bows once. Yumiko changes the sakaki on the 1st and 15th of each month. As a toddler Mizuki thought the clap switched the hotplate on.
- **Fukuzasa:** every January at Toka Ebisu (Imamiya Ebisu, 9 to 11 January) the family buys a lucky bamboo branch for business and hangs it above the till; last year's goes back to the shrine. Isamu took her as a small child and hoisted her up to see; from 1995 Yumiko took her.
- **Rites of passage:** miyamairi at Sumiyoshi, May 1990 (Chiyo carried her, as the paternal grandmother does; Fumiko cried). Shichi-go-san at Sumiyoshi at 3 (10 November 1993) and 7 (November 1997, red kimono, ate the whole bag of chitose-ame before the photo); Hiroshi photographed both on the FM2.
- **Hatsumode** at Sumiyoshi every New Year: the drum bridge (Sorihashi), temizu (left, right, mouth, ladle upright), offering box, bow-bow-clap-clap-bow. Exception: 2017, a mourning year, when she went alone in late January after the fifty days. From Sydney she joins by video call while the family is at Sumiyoshi. **Omikuji** every year: bad ones tied to the rack, good ones kept in her wallet. **Daikyō** (great curse) in 2017, the breakup year (ch10; mention gently, rarely). Aya always draws "kichi" and finds it boring.
- **Nagoshi no harae, 31 July (Sumiyoshi does it a month after most shrines):** the chinowa grass ring at Sumiyoshi in a figure of eight with Fumiko, every year from 1994 while Fumiko could walk it.
- **Omamori:** study charm from Osaka Tenmangu, January 2009 (from Fumiko, with the ema). At Kansai in March 2018 Yumiko gave her two: the Shitennoji paper charm for her wallet (stolen at Central in April 2018; replaced by post) and a Sumiyoshi kōtsū anzen (travel safety) charm, tied to her backpack beside the Vulpix keyring. The Vulpix later moved to the FM2 strap; the travel charm stays on the backpack, replaced on each trip home (2023, 2025) with the old one returned to Sumiyoshi.
- **Yakudoshi:** 33 by traditional counting, 2022 (2021 and 2023 either side). Yumiko posted a Sumiyoshi yakuyoke charm in January 2022 and blamed everything that year on it, including Mizuki's COVID. On the 2023 trip home Mizuki and her mother went to Sumiyoshi to give thanks it was over.
- **Kenta's wedding** (late October 2021, shrine in south Osaka): she watched the san-san-kudo sake sharing by phone and took her own nine sips in Pyrmont.
- **Sydney:** the Sumiyoshi ofuda her mother posts every December (since 2018; on the Glebe wardrobe, then from May 2021 on a very small shelf on top of her Pyrmont bookshelf) with a small cup of water. She claps there on New Year's Day and before big things: flights home, the night before her first call to Scott (11 November 2026), and (planned) her first market enquiry for the stall. No Sydney shrine. Setsubun in the flat: beans thrown in a whisper ("oni wa soto, fuku wa uchi") because of the neighbours, one bean for each year of her age plus one, then eho-maki.
- **Everyday habits:** itadakimasu and gochisōsama at every meal; mottainai (hates waste); bows to the sea before swimming (from Hiroshi, Shirahama, 1998); never steps on the edge of a tatami (from Fumiko).
