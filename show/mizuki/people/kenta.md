# Kenta Tanaka

Mizuki's younger brother. Character page: keep him consistent with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md` (which wins any conflict) and the chapters in `show/mizuki/history/`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 22 November 1993, Abeno, Osaka. Turns 33 on 22 November 2026. The date is "ii fūfu no hi", Japan's "good married couple day" (11/22), which their mother takes credit for.
- **From:** Abeno, Osaka. Grew up in the rooms above the shop, sharing a room with Mizuki.
- **Job:** systems engineer at an IT company in Osaka, working on payment and booking systems for shops. Office near Umeda, at home two days a week since 2020.
- **Lives:** a flat in Tennoji ward, ten minutes' walk from the shop, with his wife Miho (née Sato, married autumn 2021) and their daughter Hana (born 14 April 2024).
- **Relationship to Mizuki:** her little brother, three and a half years younger. She is his onee-chan, and has been his "manager, translator and comedy partner" since he could dribble.

## Looks and style

Taller than everyone else in the family, which Mizuki considers a betrayal. Slim, slightly round-shouldered from screens, black-framed glasses, short hair he started cutting himself, badly, in 2020 and hasn't stopped since. Wears the same thing every day: dark chinos, a plain navy or grey shirt, a black backpack, white trainers. Weekends: a Dragon Quest slime T-shirt Miho has tried to throw away twice. Since Hana, permanently a little tired and a little sticky.

## Personality and sayings

Quiet, practical, dry, the boke to Mizuki's tsukkomi as children and still. Kind in useful ways rather than in words. Hates fuss, loves a working system. Says little and means it, like their father. Sends photos without words; a thumbs-up is his idea of a long reply.

Quotes Mizuki repeats:
- "Nee-chan, fingers?" (Pausing any violent film at New Year to check she's covering her eyes.)
- "Does Santa wear shorts in Australia?" (Christmas 2018 video call.)
- "It's only a menu, nee-chan. Press the button." (Teaching their parents, and her, technology.)
- "Slime is the strongest monster. It never gives up."
- "Bianca. Obviously." (Dragon Quest V's bride choice; see below.)
- The 5am message on 14 April 2024: one photo of Hana, no words. "The most Kenta thing he has ever done."

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** Dragon Quest above all things (he was Dragon Quest to her Final Fantasy: "not a peaceful household"); Super Mario Kart, which he learned to win at because he wasn't allowed to cry; Weekly Shōnen Jump, which he bought every Monday from pocket money and she read second; Mini 4WD racing cars as a boy; spreadsheets; trains (quietly; he can name every Midosuji line station in order and backwards); curry; his father's squid okonomiyaki; Hanshin Tigers, mildly; Miho's cooking; Hana, completely.
- **Dislikes:** fuss and speeches; long phone calls (video calls only, cameras on, five minutes); Final Fantasy's "dramatic hair"; being called "Ken-chan" by the regulars; green peppers (the one thing he and Mizuki fully agree on); Sephiroth, out of loyalty to her; losing at anything.

## Life story

- **1993** born 22 November. Mizuki, three, is not impressed. Within a year she has appointed herself his manager.
- **1996** at two and a half, eats her pudding; she names her Pokémon rival after him.
- **Late 1990s** plays the sprite to her girl in Seiken Densetsu 2 at Fumiko's; she screams at him for healing the rabbit. Learns to win at Super Mario Kart. Gets told "in the name of the moon, I'll punish you" every time he steals a snack.
- **1999** scoops a goldfish at the Aizen festival and names it, for reasons never explained, Tanaka. Tanaka the goldfish lives six years.
- **2000** Dragon Quest VII (August, PlayStation) is his first; Mizuki is FF, so he declares for DQ, and that's that.
- **2002** pulls the TV plug and the aerial out for Mizuki after she sees Ringu.
- **2004** Dragon Quest V, remade for PS2 (March): he picks Bianca and argues about it with every classmate. Dragon Quest VIII (November) arrives five days after his eleventh birthday.
- **2005** Tanaka the goldfish dies, the same year as Grandpa Isamu. A hard year for an eleven-year-old; he goes very quiet and builds Mini 4WD cars for weeks.
- **2009** Dragon Quest IX on the DS: he and his friends walk around Den Den Town to swap maps by passing other players.
- **2012 to 2016** studies information engineering at a university in Osaka (his first choice; Center Test January 2012). Joins his IT company as a systems engineer in April 2016, aged twenty-two.
- **Spring 2017** moves into a small one-room flat of his own near his office, the first Tanaka child to live anywhere but above the shop. Plays Dragon Quest XI there that summer. In the winter gives Mizuki the Super Famicom Mini.
- **2017** checks Mizuki's Australian visa application with her and teaches their parents to video call: "one of the most important things anyone did for me in the next ten years", Mizuki says.
- **2018** at Kansai Airport makes a terrible joke at the gate that makes her laugh instead of cry.
- **Autumn 2018** meets Miho Sato through a colleague. **New Year 2021** sends the family chat a photo of an engagement ring, without a word. **Late October 2021** marries her at a shrine in south Osaka, close family only (and Aya), in a black haori; Mizuki watches on a phone in Sydney, and at the end he takes the phone and bows to his sister.
- **2023** starts driving his father to the Wakayama rocks for fishing after Hiroshi's slipped disc.
- **2024** Hana born, 14 April. He sends one photo at 5am.
- **2024** Dragon Quest III HD-2D (November). He plays it in ten-minute pieces while Hana naps.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** Secret of Mana at Fumiko's ("Heal ME! Why are you casting on the rabbit?"); being the straight man who couldn't answer back; the shared room above the shop and the unplugged TV; "Nee-chan, fingers?"; One Piece fights over who creased the Jump cover; checking her visa forms in 2017; the airport joke; watching his wedding through a phone propped against her shiso pot in 2021; the 5am photo of Hana; on her 2025 trip, the two of them standing at the shop counter late at night eating broken okonomiyaki like children.

**How they talk now:** rarely in words. A LINE message every week or two, usually a photo with no caption: Hana, a shop sign, a bowl of ramen he thinks she'd judge, the newest Pokémon game (she watched him play it on a video call and was "happy to leave it there"). He calls when something needs fixing: her phone, their parents' tablet, a form. He reminded her that Seiken Densetsu 2 is on Switch in Collection of Mana. Miho sends the words he doesn't.

**What she worries about:** that he works too late and is turning into their father, saying nothing about being tired; that he carries the family now, living ten minutes from the shop while she's in Sydney, and never complains; that she owes him for 2023. She tells him so. He sends a thumbs-up.

## Running details for the show

- **Dragon Quest vs Final Fantasy:** the household war. His favourite is Dragon Quest V; her line is "a game where the big romantic choice is a menu". His rebuttal: "A game where the hero's hair weighs more than the sword."
- The slime T-shirt and a slime keyring on his backpack. Hana's first soft toy from him: a slime.
- He watches action films at New Year and always checks: "Nee-chan, fingers?"
- He almost never uses an emoji beyond a thumbs-up. Their mother thinks he's angry; he isn't.
- Kenta can't cook. He makes exactly one dish, curry from a roux block, "the way the box says".
- Birthday: 22 November, "good married couple day". Mizuki sends him a TWICE song every year because he hates it.
- He's the family's IT department: set up the shop's QR payments for Yumiko and the video calls for everyone.
- He is the one who would queue for a Switch 2 for her in Umeda, if it came to that.
