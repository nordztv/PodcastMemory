# Hiroshi Tanaka

Mizuki's father. Keep consistent with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md` (which wins) and the chapters in `show/mizuki/history/`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 3 August 1958, Abeno ward, Osaka. Turned 68 in August 2026.
- **From:** Abeno, Osaka, all his life. Only child of Isamu and Chiyo Tanaka.
- **Job:** second-generation okonomiyaki cook. Runs the hotplate at the family shop his father Isamu opened in Abeno in 1964: eight counter seats, two tables, a noren curtain, one big black iron hotplate he seasons "the way other men polish cars". Closed Wednesdays.
- **Lives:** still in the two small rooms above the shop with Yumiko.
- **Married:** Yumiko Morita, Sunday 8 November 1987.
- **Relationship to Mizuki:** her father. She is his "boss's boss's daughter", he says (Yumiko is the boss).

## Looks and style

Medium height, solid forearms, a cook's stoop that got worse after the 2023 slipped disc. Short grey hair. A white towel tied round his head at the hotplate, a navy apron, a white T-shirt in summer, a grey sweatshirt in winter. Off duty: a faded Hanshin Tigers cap (never washed "in case the luck comes out") and sandals. His face in photos is always serious: "his happy face, just with the volume off".

## Personality and sayings

A deadpan boke who says absurd things in a flat voice and lets Yumiko catch them. Says very little, notices everything, shows love sideways and with a joke (the PlayStation 2 "for watching DVDs", Christmas 2001). Stubborn about cabbage.

Quotes Mizuki repeats:
- "Mada mada." ("Not yet, not yet." After a bad Tigers inning, and about most things.)
- "The Tigers teach you patience. And humility. And how to drink beer slowly."
- "The light meter still works. The rest is up to you." (Giving her the FM2, 2006; "the longest speech about feelings he made in my entire teenage life".)
- "Take the camera." (When she told him she was going to Australia, 2017.)
- "Tokyo glue." (Natto.)
- "Too much cabbage." / "That's a good one." (Her okonomiyaki on 2020 video calls.)
- "Good." (Her university results, 2009; then a free okonomiyaki for every customer.)
- "Kakkoii." (One word, about her Nikon Zf next to the FM2, 2024: the highest praise she's ever had from him.)
- "Same at the hotplate. Talk to the regulars, then let them eat." (When Mizuki repeated Ms Kuroda's "talk to them, then stop talking" at dinner in 2006. Both taught it to her.)

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** the Hanshin Tigers above all; Randy Bass, the American slugger of the 1985 championship side; Southern All Stars (he was nineteen when "Katte ni Sinbad" came out in June 1978 and still says "Itoshi no Ellie" is the best song ever written); old enka (Ishikawa Sayuri's "Tsugaru Kaikyo Fuyugeshiki"); rock fishing (iso-zuri) off the Wakayama coast for gure, the Kansai name for the opaleye fish; a cold beer after closing; quiet.
- **Dislikes:** natto; anyone pressing an okonomiyaki down; the Yomiuri Giants; monjayaki ("okonomiyaki that gave up", Mizuki's line, which he's repeated to customers for twenty years); Mr Ishida's Hawks; long speeches; doctors; being fussed over.

## Life story

- **1958** born 3 August in Abeno, the only child of Isamu and Chiyo.
- **1964** aged six, watches his father open the shop. Grows up in the two rooms above it. Isamu supports the Nankai Hawks, but Hiroshi is a Hanshin Tigers boy from the start, like most of Kansai; as a teenager he sits in Koshien's right-field stands and discovers a quiet person can shout.
- **1977** finishes high school and works at the shop full-time, for "meals and pocket money".
- **1982** his father finally puts him on proper wages. With the first of them he buys a **Nikon FM2** (new that year), half thinking he might be a photographer. He photographs the shop, the regulars and, later, a young woman from a department store who keeps coming in after work.
- **1985** in spring, a young woman from the Kintetsu department store sits at the counter and he burns an okonomiyaki for the only time in his career ("Is this a joke?" "It's well done."). That year the Tigers win the Japan Series; fans throw the Colonel Sanders statue into the Dotonbori canal, and he blames them for every bad season since (it was finally fished out in 2009: "Now they have no excuse.").
- **1987** marries Yumiko on 8 November; his speech is one sentence: "Thank you for coming, please eat a lot." He becomes Isamu's partner at the hotplate, and the newlyweds take the rooms upstairs.
- **Around 1994** Isamu hands him the hotplate fully (the spring Mizuki starts kindergarten) and comes in every morning at ten to "supervise" from seat one.
- **1990** Mizuki born; he asks the nurse whether babies can eat okonomiyaki yet. **1993** Kenta.
- **Early 1990s** starts eating at Mr Ishida's ramen shop near Namba once a month, in silence, to argue baseball. A happy feud for thirty years.
- **1995** after the earthquake he drives the shop van towards Kobe with water and rice balls for his cousin's family, gone two days, and brings them back to Fumiko's.
- **2003** Tigers win the pennant; he stands at the hotplate with folded arms and wet eyes. They lose the Japan Series to Mr Ishida's Daiei Hawks in seven games.
- **2005** his father dies suddenly in February. He pulls the shutter down for a week. At the crematorium he says one sentence to Mizuki: "He'd have said the incense was too thick." When the shop reopens he cooks with Isamu's old spatulas and puts the first okonomiyaki of the night on a plate on seat one, with a can of coffee.
- **2006** gives the FM2 to Mizuki.
- **2023** late March, Mizuki home: he says nothing and makes her a pork and kimchi okonomiyaki on the third seat from the left. **May**: slipped disc; shop closed two weeks, the longest in its history. **November**: the Tigers win the Japan Series against Orix, the first since 1985; on the video call he holds up a towel and says almost nothing, "which for him was screaming".
- **2025** Tigers win the pennant again and lose the Japan Series to the SoftBank Hawks. Mr Ishida's 2026 New Year card to Mizuki: "Tell your father."

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** carrying her over the drum bridge at Sumiyoshi Taisha on his shoulders every New Year until she was five; lifting her to rub Billiken's feet; Sailor Mercury sound effects while scraping the hotplate; one extra strand of beni shoga to watch her face; the PS2 "for DVDs"; the FM2 between the dinner bowls; a roll of her at graduation in 2013, "nervous for the only time I've seen him hold a camera"; pork and squid the night before she left.

**The kamidana:** every morning before opening, on an upturned beer crate (more carefully since 2023), he puts out water, salt and rice on Isamu's kamidana, then bows twice, claps twice, bows once and lights the gas. Toddler Mizuki thought the clap switched the hotplate on; in 2025 she clapped along from the stairs and he said, without turning round, "Still switches it on." He also gave her the habit of bowing to the sea before swimming (Shirahama, 1998: "It's bigger than us").

**How they talk now:** almost never directly. He appears behind Yumiko on the Sunday video call, says one dry thing and leaves. When the Tigers win, Mizuki sends a single tiger emoji and he sends one back: "That's our whole conversation, and it's perfect." Out of season he sends a photo of a fish he caught, no caption, maybe twice a year.

**What she worries about:** his back, and that he won't admit it; that he still lifts the cabbage crates himself; that he and her mother will run the shop until they drop, because neither knows how to stop; that there's no third generation for the hotplate (Kenta doesn't cook). She is saving to bring her parents to Sydney (hoped for September 2027); he has never been on a long flight and says he'll go "if the Tigers are already out of it".

## Running details for the show

- **Tigers habits:** won't watch the ninth inning if they're leading by one ("it's my fault if I look"); the same towel, unwashed, in a drawer under the till; the 2023 slogan "A.R.E." ("that thing", meaning the championship, buzzword of the year in 2023) still makes him smile; never says the score of the 2005 Japan Series out loud (Tigers lost all four games to Chiba Lotte). Every Tigers win: one tiger emoji.
- He takes the shop van to the Wakayama rocks two or three times a year, leaving at 3am; since the 2023 back, Kenta drives and sleeps in the van.
- Southern All Stars on the radio means a good mood; enka means he's thinking.
- Through Mizuki he swaps gifts with Mr Kondo, whom he's never met: shop sauce to Sydney, Shiroi Koibito biscuits to Abeno. His review: "Good biscuit."
- Once a year, at the New Year's Eve family karaoke, he sings exactly one song: "Itoshi no Ellie". Badly. Everyone has to be silent.
- Thinks a flat white is "milk with ambitions".
- The standing mat in front of the hotplate was bought with the money Mizuki sent in 2023. He calls it "Mizuki's mat".
