# Daisuke Tanaka

Mizuki's second cousin, always just called her "cousin". Character page: keep him consistent with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md` (which wins any conflict) and the chapters in `show/mizuki/history/`. The 1995 earthquake is part of his story: mention it gently and briefly, never for comedy. Everything after it (the Super Famicom, FFVII, the theme parks) is warm, funny material.

## Basics

- **Born:** 18 July 1985, Kobe. Turned 41 in July 2026.
- **From:** east Kobe (Higashinada ward, in a flat near the sea), Hyogo Prefecture.
- **Family tree:** son of **Makoto Tanaka**, Hiroshi's first cousin, and **Keiko Tanaka**. Makoto's father, **Tsutomu**, was Isamu's younger brother. Makoto moved to Kobe for work and works for a shipping company at Kobe port: a quiet man. Keiko is cheerful and round-faced, learned to make modan-yaki in the Tanaka shop in 1995, and wears a sun visor in all weathers.
- **School:** elementary from April 1992, junior high from April 1998, high school from April 2001, university (commerce, in Kobe) April 2004 to March 2008.
- **Job:** accountant at a mid-sized accounting firm in Sannomiya, central Kobe. Busy season every February and March, when Japan's individual tax returns are due (the filing period runs from mid-February to mid-March).
- **Lives:** a flat in Kobe's Nada ward with his wife **Yuka** (born 1987, a pharmacist's assistant, married 2012) and their two sons, **Sota** (born 2014) and **Riku** (born 2017).
- **Relationship to Mizuki:** second cousin; "something like a brother" since the spring of 1995. He still calls her **"the audience"**.

## Looks and style

Was a skinny, serious nine-year-old; is now a tall, slightly soft, very tidy man in rimless glasses, with early grey at the temples he blames on tax season. Navy suit and a sensible tie on weekdays; on weekends a fleece, chinos and an Orix Buffaloes cap. Carries a notebook and a mechanical pencil everywhere. Smiles with his mouth closed until something is genuinely funny, then laughs like a much younger man.

## Personality and sayings

Quiet, careful, patient, the kind of person who reads the manual. Explains things slowly and well, which he first did for a four-year-old girl with a second controller. Kind without fuss. Kobe-proud: a little more polished than the Osaka Tanakas, and they never let him forget it. A gamer for life, and the one who introduced Mizuki to Final Fantasy, for which she both thanks and blames him.

Quotes Mizuki repeats:
- "The audience is here." (His greeting to Mizuki since 1997, because she watched him play FFVII and wasn't allowed to touch the controller.)
- "It's only a game." (After Aerith's death in 1997, before going very quiet himself. She has never fully forgiven it.)
- "Look. Jump here. Again." (Teaching her Super Mario World, 1995.)
- "So the little ones have something to do." (Leaving the Super Famicom at Fumiko's.)
- "The audience is back." (His one-line message in April 2020, when he heard Kenta was playing Final Fantasy VII Remake for her on video call; it came out on 10 April, the birthday she should have spent in Osaka. She printed it and stuck it in her journal.)
- "It was never mine after 1995. It's yours. The audience gets the console." (When she rang about the Super Famicom in 2016.)

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** Final Fantasy VII, forever (and Remake and Rebirth); Super Famicom-era games; Ocarina of Time, which he played at Fumiko's on his Nintendo 64 with Mizuki watching; the Orix Buffaloes, since 1995 (see below); Kobe's own things: Kobe beef he can't afford, Nankinmachi's pork buns, the harbour at night, the Rokko mountains; a clean spreadsheet; his sons' Pokémon battles; the Kobe Luminarie lights every winter (since 2024 they're held in January); trains to anywhere.
- **Dislikes:** being called "Osaka" by anyone outside Kansai; tax season; spoilers; the Yomiuri Giants (the one point of family unity); people who skip cutscenes; loud bars; earthquakes on the news (he goes quiet, like Mizuki).

## Life story

- **1985** born 18 July in Kobe. **1987** aged two, spends Hiroshi and Yumiko's wedding reception under a table eating prawn crackers.
- **17 January 1995** aged nine, his family's flat in east Kobe is badly damaged in the Great Hanshin earthquake. They are safe. Hiroshi drives the shop van towards Kobe with water and rice balls, leaves it at Ashiya, walks the rest, and on the 19th brings them back to Osaka. Fumiko, no blood relation, takes them in until spring: "I have a spare room and a big pot." The one thing he insisted on carrying out of the flat, in his school rucksack wrapped in a jumper, is his Super Famicom with two controllers and three cartridges (Super Mario World, Super Mario Kart, Seiken Densetsu 2). He is temporarily enrolled at the local elementary school in Abeno. For weeks he barely speaks; he first laughs at Setsubun, 3 February, when Hiroshi in the demon mask asks for a cup of tea. In the evenings he plays, and a bossy four-year-old sits behind him. One evening in February he hands her the second controller and lets her be Luigi. He starts talking again.
- **Late March 1995** Makoto finds a rented flat in Tarumi, in west Kobe, while their building is repaired, and the family goes home. At the station Daisuke shakes Mizuki's hand like a grown man, then comes back to tell her he's left the Super Famicom at Fumiko's, cartridges in a biscuit tin, "so the little ones have something to do". He never collects it. That autumn the Kobe baseball team, the Orix BlueWave, with Ichiro and the slogan "Ganbarō Kobe" ("Hang in there, Kobe"), wins the Pacific League pennant; they win the Japan Series in 1996. He has been an Orix fan ever since.
- **1997** FFVII (out 31 January). Over the spring holidays at the end of March, aged eleven, he brings his PlayStation to Fumiko's for a few days and plays all day while nearly-seven Mizuki narrates the text boxes; she is not allowed to touch the controller, and he starts calling her "the audience". Aerith's death: Mizuki sobs, he says "it's only a game", then goes quiet. He comes back in Obon week to finish it.
- **1998 to 1999** from the winter of 1998, plays Ocarina of Time on his Nintendo 64 at Fumiko's in the school holidays; Mizuki still isn't allowed to touch the controller.
- **2000** in junior high. At Golden Week his mother Keiko takes him, Mizuki and Kenta to Expoland in Suita: he rides the stand-up coaster, she holds the bags and loves the Ferris wheel. That summer, fifteen and in his last year of junior high, he brings Final Fantasy IX and Dragon Quest VII to Fumiko's.
- **2002** spring holidays: sits behind Mizuki while she plays FFX on her own PS2: "Now I'm the audience."
- **August 2001** sixteen and in his first year of high school, in an Orix cap, comes to Universal Studios Japan with the Tanakas, months after it opened; he and Mizuki get soaked on the Jurassic Park ride, twice.
- **February 2005** comes from Kobe with his parents for Isamu's funeral and stands beside Mizuki without saying anything.
- **2004 to 2008** studies commerce at a university in Kobe. **2008** joins an accounting firm in Sannomiya. **2009** meets Mizuki after her Kobe university exam and buys her a pork bun in Chinatown. **New Year 2010** she watches him play FFXIII in Kobe.
- **2012** marries Yuka. **2014** Sota born; **2017** Riku born.
- **2016** comes to Fumiko's funeral in December in a black suit and stands next to Mizuki without speaking. Clearing Fumiko's house, Mizuki finds the Super Famicom under the television in its cardboard box and rings him. "It was never mine after 1995. It's yours. The audience gets the console." It goes into a box in Kenta's old bedroom above the shop, where it still is.
- **2020** sends "The audience is back" after her cancelled-trip birthday. **Spring 2023** comes over from Kobe one Sunday with his two sons during her first trip home: "The audience returns." **November 2023** Japan Series, Hanshin against Orix: he and Hiroshi exchange one text per game. Hanshin wins in seven. Daisuke sends Hiroshi a photo of a white flag.
- **February 2024** plays FFVII Rebirth late at night after tax returns.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the second controller and Luigi; "the audience"; Aerith; the Mana Fortress and Fumiko's flying heads; the Expoland Ferris wheel; getting soaked together at USJ in 2001, "the happiest day of the year"; the Super Famicom in the biscuit tin.

**How they talk now:** a few times a year on LINE, more around a Final Fantasy release; always on 17 January he sends a single word, "otsukare" (well done, you made it), and she sends one back. That's all either of them needs to say. Birthday messages both ways. New Year cards with photos of the boys. She sees him on trips home if he can get to Osaka; in spring 2023 he brought his sons over from Kobe one Sunday.

**What she worries about:** that he works too hard in tax season; that the anniversary every January is heavier for him than he lets on. She never asks. She just sends the word back.

## Running details for the show

- Calls her "the audience". She calls him "the player".
- FFVII Remake came out on her birthday, 10 April 2020. "Daisuke still thinks Square Enix did it for him."
- He's an Orix Buffaloes fan (Kobe's team from 1995; now based in Osaka after a 2004 merger, playing some games in Kobe still). Hanshin-Orix games are a family event.
- His sons Sota (12) and Riku (9) play Pokémon; Mizuki sends them Australian Pokémon card packs.
- Every year he takes the boys to Kobe Luminarie, the light festival that began in December 1995 in memory of the earthquake (it was held in December until the pandemic and now runs in late January).
- The Super Famicom: left at Fumiko's in 1995, never collected, given to Mizuki in 2016 ("The audience gets the console"), now boxed in Kenta's old room above the shop. The Super Famicom Mini went to Sydney with her.
