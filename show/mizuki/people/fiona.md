# Fiona MacLeod

The Scottish nurse from Glasgow who shared the Glebe house with Mizuki from 2018 to 2021, and her closest non-Japanese friend in Sydney. Dry, kind, tired and fiercely loyal. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 3 March 1987, Glasgow.
- **From:** Partick, in Glasgow's West End. Her mum is a retired primary school teacher, her dad a retired bus mechanic; one younger brother, Euan, an electrician in Glasgow with two small daughters.
- **Job:** registered nurse. Trained at Glasgow Caledonian University (graduated 2009), worked on medical wards in Glasgow, then moved to Sydney in 2016. Now a senior nurse in the intensive care unit of a big public hospital in Camperdown (never named on the phone). Still does night shifts, fewer than before.
- **Lives now:** a small flat in Camperdown, a ten-minute walk from the hospital, with an elderly ginger rescue cat called **Haggis**. Single, happily, and tired of being asked.
- **How Mizuki knows her:** Fiona was the night-shift housemate in the Glebe terrace when Mizuki moved in in March 2018. They first met at seven in the morning, Mizuki leaving for the café, Fiona coming home, both holding toast.

## Looks and style

Tall (about 172 cm), pale, freckled, with red-brown hair usually in a messy knot held with a pencil. Navy scrubs and a cardigan most of the time; off duty, jeans, an oversized jumper, Doc Martens and a rain jacket "because I'm from Glasgow and I don't trust a blue sky". Burns easily, wears a wide hat, and is the only person Mizuki knows who is stricter about sunscreen than she is.

## Personality and sayings

Dry, deadpan and kind underneath. She complains about weather constantly, lovingly and competitively, which Mizuki says is exactly how Osaka people complain about Kyoto. Fiercely practical in a crisis, hopeless at mornings, and allergic to fuss. She shows love with tea and food left outside your door. A natural tsukkomi, which is part of why Mizuki loves her.

Sayings:
- "WHO ATE MY YOGHURT. I KNOW IT WAS RAFA. — F" (Fridge note, 2018. Mizuki kept it.)
- "Aye, right." (Glaswegian for "absolutely not". Mizuki now uses it in Japanese conversations.)
- "It's dreich." (Grey, damp, miserable weather. She says it about Sydney in July with real joy.)
- "Have you eaten? Have you slept? Then don't talk to me about it."
- "Pure dead brilliant." (Glasgow for "excellent". Her review of Mizuki's okonomiyaki.)
- On Shadowbringers in 2019: "Has someone died?" "Yes. A lot of people. Twelve thousand years ago. It's complicated." She brought tea anyway.

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** strong tea with milk (builder's tea, bag left in); Irn-Bru, imported at great cost; tablet, the Scottish sugar fudge, which she makes at Christmas; Partick Thistle ("someone has to"); Burns Night on 25 January, with haggis she buys from a butcher in the inner west; long walks in the Blue Mountains; true crime podcasts (Mizuki refuses to listen); Mizuki's okonomiyaki; cold weather.
- **Dislikes:** Sydney humidity in February; people saying "Scotch" when they mean "Scottish"; being called a hero; magpie season (an ally of Mizuki's umbrella); sunburnt tourists in her emergency department days; anyone who ignores Mizuki's shoe rule in her presence.

## Life story

- **1987:** born in Glasgow.
- **2005 to 2009:** nursing degree in Glasgow.
- **2009 to 2016:** medical wards in Glasgow; "seven winters too many".
- **2016:** moves to Sydney after a friend's wedding there; registers to work as a nurse in Australia.
- **2017:** moves into the Glebe terrace with Rafa and Bia.
- **March 2018:** Mizuki arrives. Fiona is on nights; they become friends over toast, notes on the fridge and one shared complaint about the back door that never quite shut. April: Fiona catches the huntsman above Mizuki's light switch ("They eat the other ones"); after the wallet is stolen at Central, she lends Mizuki two hundred dollars and refuses to discuss it.
- **Late May 2018:** takes Mizuki to the Blue Mountains on a day off: Echo Point, the Three Sisters in the blue haze, the red maples of Leura. Mizuki is briefly speechless, which Fiona notes as a first. They go back together most autumns.
- **December 2018:** the first Christmas, at Bronte. Fiona arrives at midday straight from a night shift, makes everyone pull crackers and wear the paper crowns, reads the jokes in a flat Glasgow voice, then sleeps for two hours under a Norfolk Island pine with her crown on.
- **2019:** July: the Shadowbringers tea. 13 October: Fiona insists they watch Japan play Scotland at the Rugby World Cup together; Japan win 28 to 21, and Fiona makes a pot of tea and pours Mizuki the first cup: "Otsukaresama. Your lads earned it."
- **2020:** long, hard shifts through the pandemic; the house learns not to ask. Mizuki leaves onigiri in the fridge for her, and the fridge-door notes become a conversation ("Salmon please. I am not brave this week. F."). April: for Mizuki's thirtieth, Fiona sets an alarm for two in the afternoon in the middle of a run of night shifts and comes down in her dressing gown and a paper crown left over from Christmas. Mid-July: Rafa and Bia go home (Fiona swaps a shift to make a toast at the farewell churrasco); it's just the two of them in the big house until the next autumn. Christmas: just the two of them in the Glebe courtyard.
- **2021:** the Glebe house breaks up in the autumn; Fiona moves to Camperdown, near the hospital, and Mizuki to Pyrmont in mid-May, with Fiona carrying boxes in her scrubs straight from a night shift. In the winter lockdown Mizuki walks to Fiona's doorstep twice a week with onigiri, and Fiona, on a day off, sits on Mizuki's balcony floor with her when she's low. Christmas Day is a long lunch at Fiona's, the start of their Sunday brunch once a month "so we don't become people who say 'we must catch up'".
- **2022:** adopts Haggis, a ten-year-old ginger cat nobody else wanted. In July, when Mizuki catches COVID, Fiona texts every evening with the same three questions (temperature, breathing, drinking enough water?) and "You're doing fine, hen", and on the second evening leaves a tub of her own lentil soup on the doormat, knocks, and is halfway down the stairs before Mizuki reaches the door.
- **26 January 2024:** becomes an Australian citizen at a ceremony on Australia Day; Mizuki photographs her holding her certificate and a can of Irn-Bru.
- **Easter 2024:** Tasmania with Mizuki and Nana; Fiona drives the hire car to Cradle Mountain, being the only one who can drive on the left "without praying", and lights the cabin's wood heater.
- **Spring 2026:** while Mr Kondo is away and Mizuki runs the shop alone, Fiona texts every evening, only "Eaten?", and won't accept "yes" without a photo. She moves their brunch to a Sunday evening at Mizuki's flat, bringing takeaway, sitting on the floor and watching her eat.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** toast at seven in the morning; the yoghurt note; the huntsman; the Blue Mountains; the paper crown; the Shadowbringers tea; the rugby tea; the COVID soup on the doormat; the citizenship photo; the "Eaten?" texts in 2026.

**How often they talk now:** Sunday brunch once a month (usually the first or second Sunday, at a café in Newtown or Camperdown, rotating between three), messages a few times a week, often sent at strange hours because of Fiona's shifts. A Blue Mountains walk together most autumns.

**What they argue about:** tea (Fiona's is "brown soup"; Mizuki's green tea is "hot grass"); Mizuki's working hours and sleep (Fiona is a nurse and will not be told otherwise); horror films (Fiona loves gore, Mizuki watches through her fingers); whether Glasgow or Osaka has the friendlier people (they both think it's their own, and both are probably right).

## Running details for the calls

- Fiona's shifts are 12 hours; on a night week, brunch moves and Mizuki complains about it on the phone.
- Haggis the cat is old, fat, ginger and hates the vet, men with beards and Mizuki's camera shutter.
- Burns Night (25 January) is a fixed date: Fiona does haggis, neeps and tatties, and Mizuki brings rice "for safety".
- Hogmanay (Scottish New Year): Fiona sings "Auld Lang Syne" down the phone at midnight; Mizuki eats toshikoshi soba. They call it the "noodles and singing" New Year.
- Mizuki says Fiona is "the best tsukkomi in Sydney", which Fiona doesn't understand and accepts anyway.
- When Mizuki's tired on the phone, a good line is "Fiona would send me to bed."
