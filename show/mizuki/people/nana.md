# Nana Yoshida

Mizuki's Japanese friend in Sydney, a nail artist from Kobe and her K-pop dance class partner. The friend who says yes to everything. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 7 July 1996 (Tanabata, the star festival, which she considers a sign), Kobe.
- **From:** Nagata ward, Kobe. Her father works for a shipping company at Kobe port; her mother is a pastry chef at a patisserie in Sannomiya. One older brother, Shun, a firefighter in Kobe.
- **Job:** nail artist at a salon in Chatswood since 2018 (never named on the phone). Known for tiny hand-painted designs: cats, ramen bowls, Hanshin stripes. Saving for her own studio.
- **Lives now:** a two-bedroom apartment in Chatswood with her Australian partner **Ryan**, an electrician from the Central Coast, and a very large collection of nail polish. She is in Australia on a partner visa.
- **How Mizuki knows her:** they met on a Saturday in late May 2018 at the Japanese grocery near Town Hall, over the last box of curry roux. Mizuki was holding it and saying out loud in Osaka-ben, to nobody, "Eight dollars? Obaachan would come back as a yokai to complain," and a voice beside her said, in Kansai-ben, "Kobe obaachan would come back too. They'd go together." Within a month Nana was the friend Mizuki called when she wanted to do something and didn't want to talk herself out of it.

## Looks and style

Small (about 155 cm, the only friend Mizuki is taller than), with long hair dyed a different colour every season (2026: ash pink). Big earrings, cropped jackets, wide cargo trousers, platform trainers. Her nails are always a work of art and always changing; Mizuki has seen a full Studio Ghibli set and a set of ten tiny dumplings. Takes a selfie at every location, including the dentist.

## Personality and sayings

Bright, bubbly, fearless and generous. She says yes to everything before she knows what it is: skydiving, a 5am beach swim, a Gold Coast trip planned in one evening, a second dessert. Mizuki, who thinks about things for a week, needs a friend like that. Nana talks in Kobe-ben, a softer Kansai dialect (she says "nani shitō?" where Mizuki says "nani shiteru n?"), and is loudly proud that Kobe is not Osaka.

Sayings:
- "Ikō ikō!" (Let's go, let's go!) to every suggestion, including bad ones.
- "Kobe is not Osaka. We're Osaka with a nicer view." (Also: Kobe is "Osaka in a nice shirt"; Mizuki's reply is that Osaka is "Kobe with no manners".)
- "Trust me." (Always said right before something goes wrong. Mizuki now hears it as a warning.)
- "Hands tell the truth." (Her nail-artist philosophy, which Mizuki, who won a prize for a photo of her grandmother's hands, finds unexpectedly deep.)

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** K-pop dance, especially SEVENTEEN and LE SSERAFIM (she likes that two of the members are Japanese, and that Kazuha is from Osaka "so even you can be proud"); TWICE's Mina, who grew up in Kobe; Kobe sweets and her mother's cheesecake; Akashiyaki, the soft eggy octopus balls from Akashi dipped in dashi, which she says beat takoyaki; sobameshi, the Kobe fried noodles-and-rice from Nagata; Vissel Kobe, who won the J1 League in 2023 and 2024; Bondi on a hot Sunday (the opposite of Mizuki); karaoke; anything with glitter.
- **Dislikes:** being called an Osaka person; people who pick at their gel nails; quiet weekends; early mornings unless there's a beach involved; spiders (she does not believe Mizuki that they're mostly a rumour); Sydney nail prices for the customer ("in Japan this design would be an artwork, here it's 'just a manicure'").

## Life story

- **1996:** born in Kobe, the year after the Great Hanshin earthquake. Her parents' first flat in Nagata burned in 1995; she knows it only from their stories and the Luminarie lights every December.
- **2014 to 2016:** studies at a nail and beauty college in Kobe, works at a salon in Sannomiya.
- **2016:** comes to Australia on a working holiday visa. Spends three months picking strawberries in Queensland for her second-year visa, "the worst and best summer of my life".
- **2018:** a couple of years into Sydney, doing a nail course while working at the salon in Chatswood. Late May: meets Mizuki over the curry roux. June: Vivid with Mizuki and Bia. September: **Luna Park** with Mizuki and Bia; when Mizuki refuses to walk through the giant face at the entrance ("a demon who ate a dentist"), Nana laughs until she has to sit on a bench. New Year's Eve: says yes to Circular Quay before being asked, and tells everyone that arriving at three in the afternoon is late (it is). Meets Ryan the same year at a beach barbecue she said yes to.
- **2019:** signs them both up for a K-pop dance class in the city without asking ("You'll say no. So I didn't ask."). They learn TWICE's "Fancy" together; Nana is the better dancer and Mizuki is the better counter. **End of May:** **Melbourne** with Mizuki, a cheap early flight and a shared hotel room where Nana snores "like a little scooter": laneways, a flat white in six cafés (Nana finishing the halves), jam doughnuts at the Queen Victoria Market, a rooftop bar, and a coach tour of the Great Ocean Road in total fog. "You've come all this way to photograph nothing." "Yes. Very good nothing."
- **2020:** the pandemic closes the salon for months. Dance class goes online (Thursday nights on video call), and from June she and Mizuki swap "Kitchen Idols" dance videos all year, starting with TWICE's "More & More". She builds an Animal Crossing island with a shrine, a torii gate and, on request, an okonomiyaki stall.
- **2021:** 10 April: Mizuki's thirty-first birthday at a karaoke box in Haymarket, just the two of them; Nana films the "Love Machine" sofa choreography. Mid-May: drives the hired van for Mizuki's move to Pyrmont "like a taxi in Osaka". Moves in with Ryan; applies for her partner visa.
- **September 2022:** the **Gold Coast** trip with Mizuki and Lily, planned by Nana in one evening: Movie World and Sea World. Nana goes on every ride twice; Mizuki screams on every ride and loves it.
- **Late 2022:** they learn LE SSERAFIM's "ANTIFRAGILE" in dance class.
- **2023:** before Mizuki's first trip home in March, paints Hanshin Tigers stripes on her nails, the start of the tradition. July: skydives at Wollongong for her twenty-seventh birthday. Mizuki watches from the beach, holding the shoes.
- **Easter 2024:** Tasmania with Mizuki and Fiona, with a suitcase twice anyone's size and Tasmanian devils on her nails; carsick on the bends to Cradle Mountain. Christmas 2024 and 2025 at Bronte with Mizuki.
- **2025:** goes home to Kobe at the same time as Mizuki's Expo trip; they meet for a day in Kobe and eat sobameshi in Nagata and Akashiyaki in Akashi, and Mizuki has to admit the Akashiyaki is "very good, for a takoyaki that's lost its confidence".

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the curry roux; the Luna Park face; the Great Ocean Road in fog; the first dance class; the Kitchen Idols videos; the Gold Coast rides; holding her shoes at the skydive; the Kobe day in 2025. And one quiet one: every 17 January, the anniversary of the 1995 earthquake, they send each other a single candle emoji and nothing else.

**How often they talk now:** dance class together on Saturday afternoons during term (they take terms off when money's tight), messages every day, a Japanese-food night once a month (takoyaki against Akashiyaki, cooked at Nana's because she has a takoyaki plate and Mizuki doesn't).

**What they argue about:** Kobe against Osaka (fashion, food, who is classier, who is funnier: Osaka wins funnier, Nana admits, "but only because you're loud"); Akashiyaki against takoyaki; Bondi on a Sunday; Nana's plans ("Trust me"); and Nana always wanting to paint Mizuki's nails long, which Mizuki can't wear at the ramen shop. Compromise: short, plain nails on workdays and a small design before every trip.

## Running details for the calls

- Nana's hair colour changes each season; a good small update ("Nana is blue now").
