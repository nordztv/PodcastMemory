# Kazu (Ueda Kazuhiro)

Leader of the **Tsukimi Noodles** Free Company in Final Fantasy XIV, a Japanese dad in Sydney's north-west, and the Roegadyn tank Mizuki has healed through most nights since 2018. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 1979, Shizuoka.
- **From:** Shizuoka city, between the green tea fields and the sea, with Mount Fuji on clear days; his family grew tea.
- **Job:** civil engineer with a transport infrastructure firm in Sydney (never named); worked on rail projects in the north-west.
- **Lives now:** a house in Kellyville, in Sydney's north-west, with his Australian wife **Karen** (a primary school teacher he met in 2006) and their two children, **Ken** (born 2011) and **Mia** (born 2014).
- **How Mizuki knows him:** in mid-2018, playing alone after her old Osaka raid group drifted apart, she answered a recruiting message for a small Free Company called Tsukimi Noodles. Kazu ran it. She was their healer within a week and had redecorated the FC house within a month.
- **In game:** a big, bearded Roegadyn Warrior called **Kitsune Udon** (a fox-noodle tank in a moon-noodle Free Company, "because the menu needed variety"). The Free Company plays on a Japanese home world.

## Looks and style

In real life: tall for a Japanese man (about 180 cm), broad, short hair with grey coming in at the sides, rectangular glasses, a gentle face. Polo shirts and cargo shorts all year, a Shimizu S-Pulse cap on weekends. A dad's backpack full of snacks, sunscreen and plasters. In game: horned helmet, enormous axe, and a glamour he hasn't changed since 2019, which Mizuki calls "the same shirt every day, but in armour".

## Personality and sayings

Steady, patient, a little shy, and very organised: a natural leader who never seems to be leading. He runs the FC like a good school excursion: on time, everyone counted, nobody left behind. He apologises in two languages. He takes damage for everyone and complains about none of it, which Mizuki, as the healer, finds both noble and exhausting.

Sayings:
- "Yoshi, ikou." (Right, let's go.) Before every pull.
- "Otsukaresama deshita." (Thanks for your hard work.) At the end of every raid night, even the terrible ones, in the old Japanese way.
- "Healer, gomen." (Sorry, healer.) Whenever he pulls too much, which is often.
- "Nine o'clock, school night." (In the early years, when he logged off at nine sharp.)
- "We live in a better house now. Thank you, Mizuki-san." (In party chat, after she redecorated the FC house in 2018.)
- "Shall we try once more?" (After the fifteenth wipe, so calmly that nobody can be angry.)

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** Shizuoka green tea (he brings his own to every meet-up and says Australian tea bags are "dust in paper"); Mount Fuji; Shimizu S-Pulse; tsukimi soba, the noodle with a raw egg like a full moon; Warrior, because "Warrior heals himself"; dungeons from A Realm Reborn; teaching new players; Sunday barbecues; trains (he is very proud that the Sydney Metro in the north-west runs without drivers).
- **Dislikes:** raids that run late; people who are rude in Duty Finder; cold houses; Sydney traffic on the M2; anyone who makes a new player feel slow.

## Life story

- **1979:** born in Shizuoka.
- **2002:** civil engineering degree; works for a construction company in Japan.
- **2005:** comes to Sydney on a working holiday "for one year".
- **2006:** meets Karen at a language exchange in the city. Stays.
- **2008:** marries Karen; begins work as an engineer in Sydney.
- **2011 and 2014:** Ken and Mia are born.
- **2013:** starts FFXIV with A Realm Reborn on a Japanese home world, so he can play in Japanese "for the kids' sake" (he says).
- **2016:** founds **Tsukimi Noodles**, named after the first Japanese meal Karen ever ate, tsukimi soba at a station stall in Shizuoka. A handful of players: Japanese people living in Australia and a few Australians learning Japanese. A tiny house in the Lavender Beds, decorated like a soba shop.
- **Mid-2018:** Mizuki joins; becomes the main healer within a week; redecorates within a month ("the lighting was a crime"): lanterns, a noodle-shop counter, a back garden with a stone lantern. Kazu walks his Roegadyn in, looks around for a long time, and thanks her.
- **2018 to 2021:** with the children small, Kazu logs off at nine sharp on school nights, because bedtime stories are his job. In the 2020 lockdowns he homeschools both children and logs on each night "with the voice of a man who had explained fractions eleven times", still logging off at nine ("rules are rules, even when there is no school"). The FC is how Mizuki keeps speaking Japanese through 2020; that winter she redecorates the house as a winter onsen ("No shoes, no tank stance"), and Kazu says it's the best thing anyone has done for him all year.
- **2019:** by the end of the year, two early-evening raid nights a week, done before his nine o'clock curfew. July: Shadowbringers; his son, allowed to stay up for the FC party, says "omedetou" on voice chat and runs away.
- **December 2021:** Endwalker launch; queues for hours on voice chat with half the FC, complaining in two languages.
- **January 2022:** when the Oceanian data centre (Materia) opens, a few Australian members move to it; Tsukimi Noodles stays on its Japanese home world.
- **2022:** with the kids old enough to read their own bedtime stories, he moves raid nights to Tuesdays and Thursdays at nine Sydney time, so Japanese members can join after dinner and Australians after their kids are in bed.
- **2023:** the first person in the FC Mizuki tells when she books her trip home: "Bring us back some Osaka." July: at the Dawntrail announcement, says very calmly, "Holiday."
- **July 2024:** Dawntrail launch party in the FC house; stays up until one, which the FC considers an act of great personal courage.
- **2025:** Ken, now fourteen, joins as a Lalafell Dancer and is allowed one raid night a week.
- **Boxing Day 2025:** logs on in a Santa outfit, the size of a wardrobe.
- **Spring 2026:** runs the Tuesday raids without Mizuki while she runs the shop alone, and tells the FC she is "on a solo duty".
- **14 November 2026:** the FC's watch party for the FFXIV live show, Saturday 10am, in the FC house (planned).

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** her first night in the FC, when he typed "yoroshiku" and gave her the healer's seat; the redecorated house and the winter onsen; the first offline meet-up in Parramatta Park in December 2018, where he poured her a cup of Shizuoka tea from a thermos and she nearly cried at the taste; running old raids for fun through 2020; the Endwalker queue.

**How often they talk now:** Tuesday and Thursday raid nights, and FC chat most evenings. They meet in person once or twice a year at the FC barbecue (every March, in a park in the north-west) and at the FC's Christmas dinner. Karen and Mizuki get on well; Karen calls Mizu Nyan "the cat who keeps my husband alive".

**What they argue about:** pulls (he pulls too much, she says; she heals too early, he says); the angles of the FC house furniture; whether raids should end at 10:30 sharp (his rule) or "after one more try" (hers); and whether Warrior "needs" a healer at all. She once let Kitsune Udon die "to teach him humility" and has never admitted it was on purpose.

## Running details for the calls

- Kitsune Udon is always the first to die and the first to apologise.
- The FC's raid times shift with daylight saving: Sydney is one hour ahead of Japan in winter and two in summer, and Kazu posts a reminder every October and April that nobody reads.
- His Shizuoka tea thermos appears at every meet-up.
- Ken, the teenage Dancer, calls Mizuki "Healer-san".
- Good on-the-phone lines: "Kazu pulled the whole room again"; "Kazu said 'otsukaresama' and I felt like I'd been to work twice"; "the FC house lighting is still correct."
