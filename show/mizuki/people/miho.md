# Miho Tanaka

Mizuki's sister-in-law, née Sato, Kenta's wife. Character page: keep her consistent with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md` (which wins any conflict) and the chapters in `show/mizuki/history/`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 7 June 1995, Sakai, Osaka Prefecture. Turned 31 in June 2026.
- **From:** Sakai, the old port city just south of Osaka. Grew up in a family house a short walk from the Daisen Kofun, the huge keyhole-shaped burial mound. Her parents still live there; her father works in the bicycle-parts trade Sakai is known for, and her mother teaches the tea ceremony to neighbours on Thursdays. One younger brother, Yuta (born 1998), who works in Osaka and plays futsal.
- **Job:** nursery teacher (hoikushi). Worked at a nursery in Sakai from April 2016, then moved to one in Tennoji ward after marrying. Maternity and childcare leave from spring 2024; back part-time since April 2025.
- **Lives:** a flat in Tennoji ward, ten minutes from the Tanaka shop, with Kenta and their daughter Hana.
- **Married:** Kenta Tanaka, autumn 2021.
- **Relationship to Mizuki:** sister-in-law. Calls her "Onee-san" in writing and "Mizuki-san" out loud, and has never once called her by anything ruder, which Mizuki finds both lovely and suspicious.

## Looks and style

Small and neat, with a round, kind face and a low ponytail. Practical clothes in soft colours: an oatmeal cardigan, a long cotton skirt or wide trousers, canvas trainers, a tote bag with a nursery-made felt badge on it. A thin gold necklace Kenta gave her when they got engaged. Always a hair tie on her wrist and a packet of tissues in her pocket, "because I work with small people". At the wedding she wore a white kimono for the ceremony and a pale blue dress after.

## Personality and sayings

Calm, kind, unflappable, quietly funny. The family's best listener. Says what she thinks gently and exactly once. Writes thank-you cards by hand, on time. Has the nursery teacher's gift of making a tantrum feel like weather: it passes. Not a tsukkomi; she's the straight face that makes the whole Tanaka manzai funnier.

Quotes Mizuki repeats:
- "Let's see." (Before every decision. It means no.)
- "He's tired, not angry." (Translating Kenta's emoji-free messages for the family.)
- "Small people need small steps." (On toddlers, and on Kenta.)
- "Onee-san, you're doing your best." (Her standard reply when Mizuki complains about work. Mizuki finds it unbearably sweet.)
- From the wedding thank-you card, still on Mizuki's fridge: "Thank you for watching from so far away. We felt you there."

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** children's picture books, especially old Japanese ones (she reads "Guri and Gura" in voices); Ghibli films, above all "My Neighbor Totoro" ("the most accurate film about small children ever made"); baking (castella, melon bread on weekends, which wins Mizuki's heart every visit); Sakai's knife shops, as a place to take visitors; the tea ceremony sweets her mother makes; Aimyon (she and Mizuki agree); Osaka Castle park in cherry-blossom season; the beach at Suma in Kobe on a weekday; tidy cupboards.
- **Dislikes:** loud restaurants; video games with no pause button ("Hana doesn't pause"); green peppers in her own lunch (she makes nursery children eat them anyway); natto, which surprises nobody in Kansai; being the centre of attention; anyone who says nursery teaching is "just playing".

## Life story

- **1995** born 7 June in Sakai, five months after the Great Hanshin earthquake. Her parents later tell her the house shook but held.
- **2000s** grows up between the kofun moats and the old merchant streets. Sakai was a great trading port in the 1500s, home of the tea master Sen no Rikyū, and the giant burial mounds became a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2019 (the Mozu-Furuichi Kofun Group). She did her school "free research" project on the mounds and still knows too much about them.
- **2014 to 2016** studies early childhood care at a junior college in Osaka.
- **2016** starts work as a nursery teacher in Sakai.
- **Autumn 2018** meets Kenta at a small dinner set up by his colleague, her college friend. He says almost nothing all evening. She asks him one question about Dragon Quest and he talks for forty minutes. "I knew then," she says. "He only talks about things he loves."
- **2020** dating through the pandemic. **New Year 2021** engaged; Kenta announces it to his family with a photo of the ring and no words, and Miho can be heard laughing in the background.
- **Late October 2021** marries Kenta at a shrine in south Osaka, small and careful, close family only (and Aya), in a white kimono. Mizuki watches on her phone from Pyrmont. Miho sends a handwritten thank-you card with a little drawing of a koala in an apron, which has been on Mizuki's fridge ever since.
- **2022** moves to a nursery in Tennoji ward.
- **2024** Hana born on 14 April. Miho's parents give Hana a set of hina dolls, as maternal grandparents traditionally do, displayed for Hinamatsuri on 3 March.
- **Spring 2023** comes down with flu two days after Mizuki lands for her first trip home, then Kenta catches it, so they meet only on video; Miho sends a tin of melon bread to the shop "for Onee-san". In the autumn, the photo of tiny white socks.
- **2025** back to the nursery part-time in April. Meets Mizuki in person for the first time in June, the evening she lands, at the Tennoji flat, and hugs her first, "which in this family is a revolution".

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the wedding on a phone propped against the shiso pot; the thank-you card; the June 2025 hug at the flat door; that evening, Miho handing Hana to Mizuki and then quietly washing the dishes so the aunt and niece could have an hour; Miho's melon bread, sent home with Mizuki in a tin "for the plane"; a long, honest talk on the Tennoji flat's balcony about being far from family (Miho's brother had just moved to Tokyo for a year), the first time Mizuki felt Miho was a sister, not an in-law.

**How they talk now:** LINE, two or three times a week, more than Mizuki talks to Kenta. Miho sends the captions Kenta doesn't: Hana's new words, nursery stories, what the parents are really like this week ("your father carried two crates again. I saw. I told your mother."). Miho is on every family call now, usually with Hana on her knee.

**What she worries about:** that Miho does more than her share with the Tanaka parents because Mizuki is in Sydney; that she's tired; that Mizuki will be a long-distance aunt forever. She tries to repay it in parcels: Tim Tams, Australian baby sunscreen, a picture book about a wombat.

## Running details for the show

- Miho is the family's secret weapon with Yumiko: the only person who can tell her to sit down and be obeyed.
- She translates Kenta. Mizuki calls her "the Kenta-to-Japanese dictionary".
- Her nursery is where Hana learned songs Mizuki hears on video calls.
- Bakes melon bread on Sundays; Mizuki insists it beats the Tennoji bakery where she worked in 2007, and would never tell the bakery.
- Sakai facts she likes to tell visitors: Sakai knives are used by chefs all over Japan; Sakai made bicycles famous; Sen no Rikyū was born there.
- She has never played Final Fantasy and has no plans to. Mizuki respects this "the way you respect a mountain".
- She likes Aimyon's "Marigold" (2018), which was playing in the restaurant the night she met Kenta.
