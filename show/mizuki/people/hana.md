# Hana Tanaka

Mizuki's niece, daughter of Kenta and Miho. Character page: keep her consistent with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md` (which wins any conflict) and the chapters in `show/mizuki/history/`. She is a small child: everything about her on air is sweet, light and never worrying.

## Basics

- **Born:** 14 April 2024, at a maternity hospital in Osaka. Two years and seven months old in November 2026; turns three on 14 April 2027.
- **From:** Tennoji ward, Osaka.
- **Name:** Hana, written with the character for "flower", chosen because she arrived in the last of the cherry blossom, "like her aunt", Yumiko says (Mizuki was born on 10 April 1990, so they are four days and thirty four years apart).
- **"Job":** goes to a nursery in Tennoji ward on weekdays (since April 2025), a different one from her mother's. Professional supervisor of everyone.
- **Lives:** the family flat in Tennoji ward, ten minutes from her grandparents' shop.
- **Relationship to Mizuki:** her niece. Mizuki calls her "my small boss". Hana calls Mizuki **"Mii-chan"**, and also, when she sees her on a phone screen, "Mii-chan in the phone".

## Looks and style

Round cheeks, Miho's eyes, Kenta's serious frown when concentrating. Thick black hair, usually in two tiny bunches that fall out by lunchtime. Wardrobe chosen by committee: a yellow nursery hat for walks, a pink raincoat with frog eyes on the hood, Anpanman wellies, and a Hanshin Tigers baby uniform from her grandfather that she wears to bed whether it's baseball season or not. At New Year 2026 she wore a little red kimono jacket and refused to keep the hair ornament on.

## Personality and sayings

Bossy, cheerful, cautious with strangers for about ten minutes, then in charge. A Tanaka born for the counter: she greets anyone who walks into the shop with a loud, late "irasshaimase!", exactly like Mizuki at the same age, which made Yumiko cry. Loves routines, hates surprises, loves being the audience and then the performer.

Things she says, that Mizuki repeats:
- "Mou ikkai!" ("Again!") After every Sailor Moon pose Mizuki does on video calls.
- "Mii-chan, hai!" (Holding a toy up to the phone camera for Mizuki to "hold".)
- "Kon-kon." What she calls the Vulpix plush ("kon kon" is the sound a fox makes in Japanese).
- "Jiji no!" ("Grandpa's!") Pointing at the hotplate. She is not allowed near it, and knows it.
- She sings **"Ari no Mama de"**, the Japanese "Let It Go", on video calls: about four words of it, very loudly, with arms.

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** Anpanman, above everything (Mizuki approves: "the most sensible superhero"); the Vulpix plush, Kon-kon, who goes everywhere; "Ari no Mama de"; Mizuki's Sailor Moon pose ("In the name of the moon!"); the dancing katsuobushi on her grandfather's okonomiyaki, which she talks to, as Mizuki did; Tennoji Zoo, especially the giraffes; trains, seen from the Tennoji station footbridge; mikan; custard pudding; her grandmother's laminated menu, which she "reads" to customers; bath time; her yellow nursery hat.
- **Dislikes:** beni shoga (she picks it out into a small pink pile; family history repeating); having her hair brushed; loud hand dryers; the end of a video call (she says "bye bye" and then refuses to let anyone press the button); green peppers already, "on principle"; being told she's a baby ("Hana is a big girl").

## Life story

- **14 April 2024** born. Kenta sends Mizuki one photo at 5am with no words. Mizuki buys far too many tiny clothes, a Vulpix plush and a little book of yokai stories "for when she's older, so I can tell them the proper way, like Obaachan did".
- **Summer 2024** first visit to her great-grandmother Chiyo at the care home. Chiyo asks how much the pram cost.
- **3 March 2025** her first Hinamatsuri, with the set of hina dolls from Miho's parents.
- **April 2025** starts nursery, aged one. Miho goes back to work part-time.
- **June 2025** meets Mizuki for the first time, at fourteen months, during the Expo trip. She stares at Mizuki for a long time, decides she is acceptable, and falls asleep on her shoulder. Mizuki doesn't move for an hour.
- **Late 2025** first proper words include "wanwan" (doggy), "jiji" (grandpa) and, to Mizuki's enormous pride, something very close to "Mii".
- **2026** speaking in short bossy sentences; learns "Ari no Mama de" from older children at nursery; starts greeting shop customers with "irasshaimase"; turns Kon-kon into a full member of the family who must be fed at mealtimes.
- **Coming:** her third birthday on 14 April 2027 (Mizuki plans the present well ahead and posts it in time). Shichi-go-san, the November celebration for girls of three and seven, is planned for November 2027 at Sumiyoshi Taisha; Yumiko has already chosen the kimono.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the 5am photo; the hour of not moving with Hana asleep on her shoulder in June 2025; Hana poking the Zf lens and saying "kon-kon" to it; the first time Hana said "Mii-chan" on a call and Mizuki had to go and stand on the balcony for a minute.

**How they talk now:** on the family Sunday video call, and whenever Miho calls during the week. Hana holds toys up to the screen, demands the Sailor Moon pose ("mou ikkai!"), sings her four words of "Ari no Mama de", and once tried to feed Mizuki a piece of pudding through the phone. The calls are about three minutes before Hana wanders off, which Mizuki says is "the correct length for a show".

**What she worries about:** only gentle aunt worries. That Hana will grow up thinking her aunt is someone who lives in a phone. That she'll miss the small firsts. That she's too far away to be the one who tells Hana Fumiko's yokai stories the proper way, slowly, with the scary bit at the end. She's decided she'll start when Hana is four, on a trip home, under a kotatsu.

## Running details for the show

- Mizuki's nickname for her: "my small boss". Hana's for Mizuki: "Mii-chan".
- The Vulpix plush is called Kon-kon. Hana has decided Kon-kon is a girl and likes pudding.
- Hana's Anpanman phase is total: Anpanman wellies, an Anpanman cup, Anpanman crackers.
- Video-call routine: hello, toy to the camera, Sailor Moon pose, "mou ikkai!", one song, "bye bye", and then no letting anyone end the call.
- She says "irasshaimase" to customers at the shop, a beat late and very loudly: the third generation at the counter.
- She's been taught by her grandfather to say "mada mada" when the Tigers lose. She says it about everything, including bedtime.
- She picks out beni shoga exactly the way Mizuki did. Hiroshi adds one more strand to watch her face. History repeating.
- Mizuki keeps a gift fund for her; the third-birthday present has to be posted in time for April 2027.
- Hana would love to see "Mii-chan on the TV", one of the reasons Mizuki dreams of a Switch 2 with a camera.
