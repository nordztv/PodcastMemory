# Aya Morimoto

Mizuki's best friend since the first day of junior high. The tsukkomi to her boke, her tank in Final Fantasy XIV, and the person who has told the Crying Out Love tissue story at every important event of her life. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 18 September 1990, Osaka (the day after what is now Scott and Mizuki's friendship day, which Aya finds suspicious).
- **From:** Abeno ward, Osaka; same junior high as Mizuki (from April 2003). Younger of two daughters: her father drove Osaka city buses for thirty years, her mother worked the deli counter at a supermarket in Tennoji. Her older sister Kana is married and lives in Nara.
- **Job:** hospital pharmacist at a big general hospital in Osaka (never named on the phone). Studied pharmacy at a different university in Kyoto from Mizuki (2009 to 2015; pharmacy in Japan is a six-year course), passed the national pharmacist exam in early 2015 and has worked at the hospital since April 2015. Now a senior pharmacist on the wards, with night duty about twice a month.
- **Lives now:** a small two-room flat near Tanimachi 9-chome in central Osaka, twenty minutes by subway from the Tanaka shop, with her shiba inu **Mame** ("bean"), adopted as a puppy in 2020. Single, and fine about it.
- **How Mizuki knows her:** the girl in the seat behind on the first day of junior high, April 2003, who whispered "Your hair tie's got a Pokémon on it. Is that Vulpix?" and then "Good choice. Mine would be Togepi."

## Looks and style

About 163 cm, a little taller than Mizuki, and she stands like she's about to start a queue. Short bob since university, always slightly too sharp at the front because she cuts the fringe herself. Practical clothes: black wide trousers, a striped shirt, white trainers, one good trench coat for spring and autumn. At work, a white coat and her hospital ID on a Kanjani Eight lanyard that she turns round when senior doctors walk past. Off duty she wears big round glasses. Her one loud item: an enormous tote bag with Mame's face printed on it.

## Personality and sayings

Quick, loud, bossy and loyal. She is the one who makes the plans; Mizuki is the one who makes the jokes. Fumiko called them "rice and pickles". Aya talks to strangers on trains, says "let's just go" to things Mizuki would think about for a week, and smacks Mizuki's arm (lightly, manzai-style) when she says something odd with a straight face. Underneath it she is very kind and hides it: she sat in the hallway with Mizuki during Battle Royale in 2004 "because she didn't like it either", which Mizuki has never believed.

Sayings:
- "Let's just go." (Her answer to everything from Seoul to a second bowl of ramen.)
- "Baka." (Her full reply to most of Mizuki's life decisions, usually with a crying-face emoji.)
- "I'm fine! My feet are not!" (Shouted from a snowbank in Otaru, 2008. Still used whenever anything is cold.)
- "You'll regret it." (Said about Mizu Nyan's name in 2013. Repeated yearly.)
- On Mizuki's sitting habits: "You're still sitting on a cushion like a monk."
- As a pharmacist: "Did you eat? Then take it with food." She says this about everything, including bad news.

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** Kanjani Eight since their 2004 debut (her favourite member is Ryuhei Maruyama; she still calls them Kanjani Eight even though they renamed themselves SUPER EIGHT in 2024). Togepi. Tanking: she plays a Paladin "because she likes running into things first". Mame. Kushikatsu in Shinsekai with a cold beer. Spreadsheets for holidays. Gryffindor ("brave, loud, says 'let's just go'"), decided after a long and serious argument in the queue at USJ's Harry Potter area in October 2016; Mizuki is Hufflepuff. Hanshin Tigers, though less fiercely than Hiroshi.
- **Dislikes:** people who don't stand on the right side of Osaka escalators (Osaka stands on the right, Tokyo on the left, and she will explain this to tourists). Being late. Horror films (she pretends she likes them). Natto, agreeing with Hiroshi for once. Patients who stop their antibiotics early. Anyone who calls Mizuki "quiet".

## Life story

- **1990:** born 18 September in Osaka.
- **April 2003:** first day of junior high; the Vulpix hair tie; best friends by lunchtime. Joins the art club; Mizuki follows after six weeks of soft tennis.
- **2003 to 2006:** dozens of emoji mails a day on flip phones; Saturday purikura in Namba and Tennoji; Sunday shopping in Shinsaibashi and Amemura.
- **2004:** Crying Out Love in the Center of the World with Mizuki (the usher and the tissues); Battle Royale, the hallway; Kanjani Eight debut, and Aya's loyalty begins.
- **2006 to 2009:** passes into the same Osaka public high school as Mizuki (different classes most years; Aya joins the brass band, trumpet, loudly). 2007: they learn Perfume's "Polyrhythm" in Aya's bedroom and perform it badly at the class party; she sends the "second button" message when Mizuki's senpai graduates.
- **2008:** the second-year school trip to Hokkaido; in Otaru, Mizuki makes her stand on a snowbank and shout "O-genki desu ka?"
- **January 2009:** waits outside the Center Test, where Mizuki's maths goes wrong, with a can of hot milk tea and, for once, says nothing. March: blames the heaters for their crying at graduation.
- **April 2009:** starts pharmacy at a university in Kyoto; meets Mizuki on the platform with two cans of hot milk tea. They share earphones and sweets on the Keihan line whenever their timetables match.
- **2010:** Confessions in the cinema with Mizuki. Starts saying, every few months, "When Final Fantasy XIV comes back, we're playing it together."
- **Autumn 2012:** their first real wobble, when Mizuki, newly in love, cancels two Sunday shopping trips and misses a message after Aya fails a chemistry test. At their takoyaki stand in Amerikamura: "You're allowed a boyfriend. You're not allowed to disappear." They make a rule: one day a month just the two of them, still more or less kept.
- **March 2013:** Mizuki's graduation trip to Okinawa (Aya, still two years from finishing, came anyway: "I'm graduating vicariously"): she drives a rented kei car up to the Motobu peninsula with Kanjani Eight on the stereo; the whale sharks at Churaumi, where Aya is silent for five whole minutes; Mizuki burnt down exactly one side, Aya barely pink ("You look like a tanuki, but only on one side").
- **August 2013:** keeps her promise; turns up at the shop with A Realm Reborn, and Mizu Nyan is born on the PS3 in Mizuki's old bedroom. Gladiator, then Paladin. In Copperbell Mines Mizuki stands in a bomb blast and Aya has to explain to two strangers why the healer is on the floor; in the Thousand Maws of Toto-Rak Aya pulls far too much and falls, and Mizuki raises her and shouts "In the name of the moon!"
- **2013 to 2017:** three raid nights a week with Mizuki in an eight-player Japanese group.
- **2015:** graduates, passes the national exam, starts at the hospital.
- **2016:** end of May: four days in Seoul with Mizuki, Aya's idea (Myeongdong, a TWICE fan cafe, too much fried chicken; Aya secretly learns the "Cheer Up" chorus first). Early September: Your Name together at a big Umeda cinema on a Sunday afternoon, crying through the credits. October: USJ for the Harry Potter area, tickets bought by Aya; Gryffindor and Hufflepuff; they leave at half past five when the Halloween zombies come out. Winter: when Fumiko dies, Aya logs in every night and just stands with Mizu Nyan in the snow of Ishgard.
- **Summer 2017:** the first person Mizuki tells about Australia, over a parfait at a family restaurant in Tennoji. Aya says "Good", then asks whether the internet will be fast enough for raids, then, in a smaller voice, "You'll come back, though?"
- **March 2018:** a call until four in the morning the night before Mizuki leaves. Later that month, the "barefoot country" call, and the family group chat's name for Australia is born.
- **2018 to 2022:** cries when Subaru Shibutani leaves Kanjani Eight (2018); adopts Mame (2020); watches dramas "together" with Mizuki over video calls.
- **Spring 2023:** an afternoon with Mizuki under the cherry trees at Osaka Castle: "You can't share takoyaki over the internet."
- **Golden Week 2024:** her first visit to Sydney (ten days, late April to early May). Bronte beach, sunscreen on her feet under protest, the Manly ferry. Outraged to learn you are not allowed to hold a koala in New South Wales.
- **2025:** goes to the Expo in its first week ("Too many people. The ring is amazing. Myaku-Myaku is horrible. I bought three."). June: one day at the Expo with Mizuki, in the rain, complaining about the reservation app the entire time; an izakaya in Tenma until the staff stack the chairs, dinner at Aya's flat with Mame on Mizuki's feet, and karaoke in Namba.
- **2026:** plays FFXIV on a Switch 2 in bed and sends Mizuki screenshots from under the covers ("still sitting on a cushion like a monk?"). In the spring, while Mizuki runs the shop alone, sends long, calm messages at night, ending with a Togepi sticker. Still her tank whenever the time zones allow.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the Vulpix hair tie; the tissues at Crying Out Love; "I'm fine! My feet are not!"; the Polyrhythm dance; the one-sided sunburn in Okinawa; turning up with A Realm Reborn; the raise in Toto-Rak; standing in Ishgard after Fumiko; the four-in-the-morning call before Sydney; the koala outrage of 2024.

**How often now:** a long video call every Sunday afternoon (Sydney time), messages most days, and raids whenever they line up. Aya is usually the first person Mizuki tells about anything, good or bad, before her family.

**What they argue about:** whether Mizuki is ever coming home ("you've been on a working holiday for eight years"); Mizuki working too hard (Aya sends pharmacist lectures about sleep); Kanjani Eight against TWICE; who pulled the extra pack in a dungeon (Aya, always, and she blames the healer's ping). Never anything serious for long. Their only real wobble was in autumn 2012, when Mizuki disappeared into her new boyfriend; it ended at the takoyaki stand in Amerikamura with the one-day-a-month rule.

## Running details for the calls

- Mame the shiba hates the rain, the vacuum cleaner and Aya's sister's cat. Mame updates are a reliable small bit.
- Aya has night duty about twice a month; on those nights she messages Mizuki from the hospital at 3am Osaka time, 4am or 5am Sydney time.
- She always orders the same thing at the Tanaka shop: mixed modan-yaki, extra mayonnaise, which Hiroshi considers a crime he tolerates.
- She keeps a framed purikura sticker from 2004 on her fridge: both of them with drawn-on cat whiskers.
- Her nickname for Mizuki is "Mizu"; Mizuki calls her "Aya-chan" only when she wants something.
- Good on-the-phone hooks: "Aya says I'm sitting like a monk again"; "Aya pulled the whole dungeon"; "my pharmacist says take it with food".
