# Rafa and Bia (Rafael and Beatriz Souza)

The Brazilian couple from São Paulo who shared the Glebe terrace with Mizuki from 2018 to 2020. Loud, warm, always cooking, and the first people in Australia who made her feel like family. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** Rafael (Rafa), May 1987, São Paulo. Beatriz (Bia), February 1988, São Paulo. Both a few years older than Mizuki.
- **From:** São Paulo, Brazil: Rafa from the east side, near the Corinthians stadium; Bia from Pompeia, a few streets from Palmeiras.
- **Jobs then:** both on student visas. Bia studied English at a college in the city and cleaned offices three nights a week. Rafa worked on building sites in 2018, in a hi-vis shirt he wore everywhere; from early 2019 he carved picanha at a Brazilian steakhouse in the city ("the same job as the site, but people clap"), which closed in March 2020. **Jobs now:** Rafa runs a small churrasco catering business in São Paulo (weddings, birthdays, office parties); Bia teaches English online and at a language school.
- **Live now:** an apartment in São Paulo with their son **Theo**, born in 2023.
- **How Mizuki knows them:** they were already living in the Glebe house when she arrived in March 2018 and took the smallest room.

## Looks and style

**Rafa:** big, broad, curly black hair, a beard he's very proud of, in his hi-vis shirt (worn everywhere, once to a wedding, and to Christmas at Bronte "for the occasion") or a Corinthians shirt, board shorts, tongs in one hand. Laughs with his whole body.
**Bia:** small, quick, long dark hair in a high ponytail, gold hoop earrings, bright sundresses, always barefoot in the house (shoes off at the door, by Mizuki's rule, which Bia was the only one to follow happily). Dances while she cooks.

## Personality and sayings

**Rafa** is loud, funny and permanently barbecuing; he talks to everyone, including the ibis. **Bia** is warm, organised and teasing, and she dances to everything, including the washing machine. Together they argue about football all the time: he's Corinthians, she's Palmeiras, which Mizuki recognised at once from her father and Mr Ishida.

Sayings:
- Rafa: "Japanese people eat meat like it's medicine. In tiny pieces." (Said at his first barbecue with Mizuki, and many times since.)
- Rafa: "Calma, calma." (Before every disaster in the kitchen.)
- Rafa taught her "bin chickens", the Sydney name for ibis. He called her "Mizu" from the first day and "chefe" (boss) from the first week.
- Bia: "Que delícia!" (About anything Mizuki cooked, including her failures.)
- Bia, on Mizuki's leftovers container: "You are the only person who takes a lunchbox to a pub."

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes (Rafa):** picanha, coarse salt, charcoal, Corinthians, loud samba on Sunday afternoons, Mizuki's okonomiyaki ("Japanese pizza", which she has asked him to stop saying), the Glebe markets.
- **Likes (Bia):** forró dancing, pão de queijo, brigadeiros, Palmeiras, Australian beaches, learning English idioms and using them wrongly on purpose, TWICE (she learned the "TT" dance from Mizuki).
- **Dislikes (both):** Sydney trains on weekends; cold houses in winter ("Australia is a warm country with freezing houses"); Vegemite (Bia), sushi with cream cheese (Rafa, who agrees with Mizuki on this one thing).

## Life story

- **2015:** married in São Paulo.
- **2017:** come to Sydney, both on student visas. Take the big front room of the Glebe terrace.
- **March 2018:** Mizuki moves in. Rafa barbecues for her on her first Saturday. Fiona is on nights; the house becomes "the United Nations of one fridge".
- **2018:** Mizuki's **Tanaka Fridays**: okonomiyaki on a borrowed electric frying pan; Rafa puts tomato sauce on it every week; she loses that war.
- **September 2018:** Bia goes to **Luna Park** with Mizuki and Nana; Mizuki is frightened by the giant face at the entrance, and Bia takes her arm and walks her through the mouth, both of them looking at the ground. Inside, Bia dances in a queue.
- **New Year's Eve 2018:** both come to Circular Quay with Mizuki and Nana for the nine-hour wait; Rafa takes the alcohol-free zone personally.
- **December 2018:** the first Christmas, at Bronte: Rafa claims an electric barbecue before seven with a car borrowed from a man on his site, and cooks prawns, picanha and sausages in his hi-vis; Bia brings her potato salad with raisins ("I am respecting the raisins from a distance"); Fiona's paper crown; then Mizuki's strawberry shortcake in the Glebe backyard at dusk, which Bia calls the best cake she's ever had. Rafa gets burnt in a perfect hi-vis shape. Mizuki's verdict on raisins in salad has not changed.
- **June 2019:** Rafa sees Godzilla: King of the Monsters with Mizuki and says she's overthinking a film about a lizard. August: Bia brings soup when Mizuki has flu.
- **April 2020:** Mizuki's thirtieth birthday in lockdown, her flight home cancelled. Bia decorates the kitchen with paper streamers that Rafa hangs slightly too low; Rafa grills sausages and picanha in the courtyard; and together the house makes the cake, a lopsided sponge with cream Bia whips by hand because the mixer has died and strawberries in the shape of "30" (the 0 "more of an egg").
- **Mid-July 2020:** with Rafa's steakhouse closed and Bia's student visa costing money they no longer have, they fly home to São Paulo on a long, expensive route through the Middle East. A farewell churrasco in the courtyard on the last Sunday, in coats: Rafa cooks everything left in the freezer, and Mizuki eats a full bowl of Bia's raisin potato salad with a straight face, as a gift; Bia notices and cries. Rafa gives Mizuki his barbecue tongs "because you are the only one who cleans them"; Bia gives her a Brazilian wish ribbon; Mizuki gives each a paper crane with a wish inside each wing. On the last morning she makes onigiri for the plane, labelled in English and Portuguese, and hugs them at the front gate under the bare frangipani.
- **2021:** Rafa starts his catering business.
- **2023:** Theo is born. They send Mizuki a photo of him in a tiny Corinthians shirt and a tiny Palmeiras hat.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the first barbecue; "meat like medicine"; Tanaka Fridays; Bia walking her through the Luna Park face; the raisin salad; the strawberry 30 cake; the tongs, the ribbon and the cranes; the goodbye under the frangipani.

**How often they talk now:** birthday messages every year, both ways, and a few photos in between. Sydney is thirteen or fourteen hours ahead of São Paulo (Brazil no longer has daylight saving, so it's fourteen in the Sydney summer and thirteen in winter), so their messages usually arrive while Mizuki is asleep. Every 10 April she wakes up to Rafa's message, which is always a photo of a barbecue, and Bia's photo of her paper crane on its shelf in São Paulo.

**What they argue about:** barbecue (Mizuki thinks yakiniku is the correct way to eat meat; Rafa thinks yakiniku is a snack); raisins; whether she's ever coming to Brazil (she says yes, one day, "when I have fourteen hours of courage for the flight").

## Running details for the show

- She can't hear "no worries" without thinking of Rafa saying it with a São Paulo accent.
- The Glebe shoe rack was her idea and Bia's pride.
- When Brazil plays at a World Cup, she supports Brazil "for Rafa and Bia" and Japan "for everyone else", and is happy either way.
- Her Brazilian vocabulary: "obrigada", "que delícia" and "picanha". She uses all three on Scott.
- Theo calls her "Tia Mizu" (Aunty Mizu) in the birthday videos.
- A good callback for barbecue season: "Rafa would say I'm cutting it too small."
