# Lily Park

Mizuki's co-worker at the ramen shop since 2019, a Korean-Australian Red Velvet devotee, her K-pop sparring partner, her Korean tutor and the closest thing she has to a younger sister. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** 22 August 2001, Sydney (Concord Hospital, her mother likes to say, "a Sydney girl, but with Korean manners, in theory").
- **From:** Strathfield, in Sydney's inner west. Her parents came from Busan in 1998: her father, **Park Jun-ho**, is a mechanic with his own small workshop; her mother, **Kim Mi-sook**, works at a Korean grocery in Strathfield. One younger brother, Daniel (born 2005), studying IT. Her Korean name is **Park Ha-eun**; only her grandmother uses it.
- **Job:** has worked shifts at the ramen shop since 2018 (weekends at first, now about three a week) and works part-time in a design job she describes as "making PDFs prettier for money", with freelance illustration on the side. Studied a Bachelor of Design (visual communication) at UTS from 2019; graduated in 2022.
- **Lives now:** with her parents in Strathfield, saving for her own place ("Sydney rent is a horror film and I'm not watching it").
- **How Mizuki knows her:** Lily had been working weekend shifts at Mr Kondo's since 2018, so she was already there when Mizuki started in early February 2019. On day one, Lily saw TWICE's "What is Love?" on Mizuki's lock screen, said "Oh no. You're a TWICE person," and the argument has never stopped.

## Looks and style

About 160 cm, long straight black hair usually in a claw clip, thick straight fringe, tiny gold earrings all the way up one ear. At work, the shop's black apron over a black T-shirt. Off work: oversized blazers, short skirts, chunky loafers, a tote bag covered in enamel pins of her own design. Always carrying an iced americano, including in July.

## Personality and sayings

Quick, sarcastic, funny and always singing under her breath. Fiercely kind under the teasing. She taught Mizuki how to say no to a customer nicely; Mizuki taught her to fold a towel the Japanese way and bow just enough to make a difficult customer feel important. Lily is good at people in a different way from Mizuki: Mizuki is warm and polite, Lily is warm and honest. She calls Mizuki **"unnie"**, the Korean word a younger woman uses for an older sister or close older friend.

Sayings:
- "Unnie, no." (Her answer to most of Mizuki's ideas.)
- "Eol-juk-a." (Short for the Korean saying "even if I freeze to death, iced americano". Her explanation for the iced coffee in winter.)
- "The argument is the fun part." (Their 2019 truce, after "Fancy" against "Bad Boy".)
- "Banned." (Said about YOASOBI's "Yoru ni Kakeru" in 2020, when Mizuki played it for the fortieth time in the shop.)
- An impression of the noise Mizuki made in the staff room at the first pictures of the Nikon Zf (announced September 2023; Mizuki bought it in February 2024): a high, strangled "eeeh?", "a kettle with an opinion". She does it whenever a camera appears.

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** **Red Velvet** since she was thirteen (they debuted in August 2014); her favourite member is Seulgi; favourite songs "Bad Boy" (2018) and "Psycho" (2019). Also NewJeans, Korean dramas, tteokbokki from Strathfield, her grandmother's kimchi, gochujang on everything (including, once, Mr Kondo's ramen, which caused a scene), drawing cats, Korean convenience-store snacks, the Strathfield night markets, roasting Mizuki's Korean pronunciation.
- **Dislikes:** TWICE fans who say "Fancy" is better than "Bad Boy"; Japanese curry ("dessert pretending to be dinner"); customers who click their fingers; people who say K-pop is "all the same"; her mother asking when she'll get a "proper job"; being late; being called "Lily-chan" (Mizuki does it to annoy her).

## Life story

- **2001:** born in Sydney.
- **2014:** discovers Red Velvet at thirteen; "Happiness" changes her life.
- **2018:** finishes high school; starts working weekend shifts at Mr Kondo's ramen shop.
- **2019:** starts her design degree at UTS, aged seventeen, fitting the shifts round lectures. Early February: Mizuki arrives. Spring: cries when Mizuki's work visa comes through. August: carries Mr Kondo's miso broth to Mizuki when she has flu. Their great argument of 2019: TWICE's "Fancy" against Red Velvet's "Bad Boy". They agree the argument was the fun part.
- **2020:** takeaway only; Lily, nineteen and studying from her bedroom, draws **Menma**, the cartoon cat with a towel round its head like Mr Kondo, on the instruction stickers for the lids ("5. Do NOT microwave the egg (Menma is watching)"). Bans "Yoru ni Kakeru" from the shop.
- **2021:** late February: *Demon Slayer: Mugen Train* with Mizuki (cries more, denies it). Autumn: hunts down Mizuki's PS5 for three weeks and hands her the phone mid-shift: "Go." Mid-May: directs the last two hours of Mizuki's move to Pyrmont. Second lockdown: "Menma 2: The Return". November: when Mizuki becomes shift lead, calls her "Boss", half respect and half revenge.
- **2022:** July: leaves a bag on Mizuki's doorstep when she has COVID: honey, lemons, Korean porridge, Pocky and "GET WELL. RED VELVET IS STILL BETTER. — L." Late September, in her uni break: the **Gold Coast** trip with Mizuki and Nana (Movie World and Sea World), named "GOLD COAST OR DIE" by Lily. She insists they start with the biggest ride, the DC Rivals **HyperCoaster** ("I'll film it if you do"), says Mizuki's scream "had different chapters", and comes home with a folder of videos called "Mizuki screams (complete works)", which she plays at the shop for weeks; one clip is still Mizuki's contact photo. October: "Go home soon, unnie." Graduates from UTS at the end of the year; Mizuki photographs her in her gown on the FM2.
- **2023:** July: drags Mizuki and Nana to *Barbie*, all three in pink. From August, tutors Mizuki in Korean on Wednesdays, alongside her Duolingo streak, in exchange for okonomiyaki. September: invents the impression of Mizuki seeing the first pictures of the Nikon Zf (she buys it in February 2024).
- **2024:** October: after Mizuki's negatives are ruined, notices first (Mizuki stops arguing about K-pop) and brings her a bubble tea every morning for a week. December: admits TWICE's "Strategy" is "catchy, unfortunately"; two Red Velvet members, Wendy and Yeri, don't renew their agency contracts; Lily takes it very hard and Mizuki buys her tteokbokki and says nothing, the Tanaka way.
- **2025:** makes Mizuki watch *KPop Demon Hunters*; claims "Golden" as her greatest victory in their argument.
- **Spring 2026:** when Mr Kondo is away for five weeks, Lily takes extra shifts, arranges her design job round the lunch rush and takes over the till so Mizuki can be in the kitchen. She also stops arguing about K-pop for five whole weeks, which alarms Mizuki more than anything. They have their only real fight, over a roster mix-up on a Friday (Lily had a design deadline; Mizuki was too tired to listen), and make up the next morning when Lily arrives with two iced americanos.
- **November 2026:** argues that a new K-pop song beats TWICE. Still Mizuki's sparring partner.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** the lock screen on day one; "Fancy" against "Bad Boy"; Menma on the lids; the PS5 hunt; the banned song; the COVID bag; the HyperCoaster and "Mizuki screams (complete works)"; the Zf noise; the roster fight and the two iced americanos.

**How often they talk now:** most weekdays at the shop, plus constant messages, mostly K-pop links with a single word ("THIS.") or memes about customers. They eat staff noodles together after the rush. One Korean lesson a week on a Wednesday after work, which Mizuki often skips for her lazy night, and Lily always notices.

**What they argue about:** K-pop (TWICE against Red Velvet, every comeback, every year); kimchi on okonomiyaki (Mizuki's favourite is pork and kimchi, which Lily says proves Korea won); Japanese curry; Mizuki's Korean pronunciation; Lily's phone at work (Mizuki is shift lead and takes this seriously, about twice a week); and Lily's future: Mizuki thinks she's too talented to be carrying bowls, and Lily thinks that's none of her business.

## Running details for the calls

- Lily is a reliable source of "the kids these days" K-pop news; Mizuki reports her opinions and then disagrees on the phone.
- She is part of Mizuki's big goal of a Seoul trip for a TWICE concert; Lily says she'll come "only to make sure you don't embarrass Korea", and to drag her to a Red Velvet event too.
- Lily's design side hustle: she drew the logo idea for Mizuki's dream okonomiyaki stall (a cartoon Vulpix holding a spatula), and Mizuki keeps it on her phone.
- Mizuki buys Lily a Christmas present every year from her gifts fund; it's always Red Velvet related, and always wrapped in TWICE paper, on purpose.
- If something bad happens at the shop, a good small line is "Lily said, 'Unnie, no,' and she was right."
- Like a younger sister: Mizuki nags her to eat lunch, to wear a jacket and to apply for real design jobs; Lily nags Mizuki to sleep and to stop saying "back in my day".
