# Takuya Ono

Mizuki's boyfriend from summer 2012 to 2017. Kind, calm, tidy, and a little boring about anime. It ended when he took a job in Tokyo and she didn't want to go: nobody's fault. Keep him light, warm and rare on the phone. Must agree with `show/mizuki/SPINE.md`.

## Basics

- **Born:** November 1989, Kyoto.
- **From:** a quiet street in Kyoto's Sakyo ward, near the Kamo River. His father works for Kyoto city; his mother, Mrs Ono, teaches tea ceremony. He lived with his parents until he moved to Tokyo.
- **Job:** from April 2013, at a Kyoto company that makes precision parts for other companies' machines (he explained what the parts did three times; she still doesn't know: "Very small, very important. Like him"); since May 2017, a project manager at a larger company in Tokyo. Never named.
- **Lives now:** Tokyo, with his wife (married 2022). Mizuki doesn't know or ask where.
- **How Mizuki knows him:** they were paired for a debate in an English discussion class at university in April 2012, on the same side (Japan should welcome more tourists). She did the talking; he had read every government tourism statistic and recited them calmly. They won. She still claims all the credit.

## Looks and style

Tall and narrow, about 178 cm, with a slow walk and very neat hair. Navy and grey, always: plain knit jumpers, chinos, clean white trainers, a canvas tote. Wire-rimmed glasses. Always ironed. Mizuki once said he dressed "like a well-organised bookshelf", and he took it as a compliment, which tells you most of what you need to know.

## Personality and sayings

Calm, tidy, gentle and polite to the point of apologising to doors. Very Kyoto: he was the first person to make Mizuki laugh at Kyoto from the inside, explaining what people really meant when they offered you tea (though when his own mother said "You must be tired, you have such a long way to go", he insisted she only meant tired; they still disagree). Steady, good with her parents, terrible at video games, and puzzled by anything with too many characters.

Sayings:
- "It was good, but a bit complicated." (On Your Name, on the escalator down from the cinema, October 2016. Mizuki has never let it go. He had said the same about Evangelion and about watching her play FFXIV.)
- "Sorry. Snow." (Crying first, with an ironed handkerchief, at Wolf Children, July 2012.)
- "I am angry. I just say it slowly. I'm from Kyoto."
- "Futsu, please." (Normal: his noodle order at Mr Ishida's Hakata shop in September 2012. Mizuki pretended not to know him.)
- "Shall we walk a little more?" (On the Kamo River, every time.)
- "Sumimasen" to doors, chairs and once a vending machine.

## Likes and dislikes

- **Likes:** the Kamo River at dusk; pour-over coffee in old jazz kissaten; Kyoto temples in the rain; planning trips in spreadsheets; My Neighbour Totoro (the only anime he fully approved of); cycling; trains and temples; his mother's matcha.
- **Dislikes:** crowds at Fushimi Inari; loud bars; games with long menus; scary films (he watched Ringu with her once, holding a cushion); Osaka people saying Kyoto is snobbish, which he would argue about gently and at length.

## Life story

- **1989:** born in Kyoto.
- **2008 to 2009:** misses his first-choice university and spends a year at a cram school (a ronin year), so he starts university in April 2009, the same year as Mizuki. They don't meet for three years.
- **April 2012:** the English debate class. July: gets his job offer from the precision-parts company. **16 August 2012:** at Gozan no Okuribi, watching the bonfires from the Kamo riverbank, says very quietly, staring straight ahead, "I like you. Will you go out with me?" Films, walks along the Kamo, affectionate arguments about whose city is better. Meets the Tanaka family ("I don't really follow baseball"); Hiroshi puts extra pork on his okonomiyaki, and he's in. Aya interrogates him for forty minutes in a Kawaramachi café and approves "with conditions".
- **September 2012:** the "normal noodles" incident at Mr Ishida's. On her last night there, in March 2013, he orders katamen, and the whole counter applauds.
- **March 2013:** graduates with her; the two fathers stand looking at a tree for five minutes. Starts work in Kyoto in April and, two weeks in, falls asleep upright at the Tanaka counter on her 23rd birthday. They see each other on her days off, meeting halfway at Hirakata on the Keihan line, or on the Kamo River. Mrs Ono finds Mizuki's accent "so lively" and comments on her watch, then slowly softens and teaches her to whisk matcha.
- **2014:** after the hotel complaint, explains calmly that head office are following a process; he is right, and she wants to throw a cushion at him.
- **November 2015:** a trip together to **Taipei** over the Labour Thanksgiving weekend, her first time abroad: night markets (he bravely eats stinky tofu), a spreadsheet itinerary, and Jiufen in the rain, where he holds the umbrella over her camera and not over himself. He is in none of the photographs; her favourite frame, the lanterns through the mist, has a blur of his umbrella at the edge.
- **2016:** October: Your Name, her second viewing: "a bit complicated". Winter: comes from Kyoto to Fumiko's wake in a dark suit and bows to the family, low and long. Fumiko had liked him: "He apologises to doors."
- **Spring 2017:** offered a good job in Tokyo in March; asks her to come. She thinks about it a long time and realises she doesn't want to leave Osaka for Tokyo. They end it gently, sadly, without a fight, on the bank of the Kamo River, then walk to Sanjo station together; he bows, a little, and she laughs. They wish each other well and mean it.
- **May 2017 onward:** Tokyo. They stop messaging after a polite New Year greeting in 2018.
- **2022:** marries, a small shrine wedding in Kyoto, in a black formal kimono beside a bride in white. Mizuki finds out from a photo on Instagram on the bus home in April.

## With Mizuki

**Key memories:** winning the debate; the bonfires at Gozan no Okuribi; Wolf Children; the Kamo River evenings; the Futsu incident; Taipei and the umbrella at the edge of the Jiufen photo; "a bit complicated"; the quiet goodbye in 2017, which was, she says, very kind on both sides.

**How often they talk now:** they don't. They follow each other on Instagram and nothing more. When she saw the wedding photo in 2022 it was a small sting: she sat in the one balcony chair with a tub of matcha ice cream for one evening, then pressed "like" the next morning, and that was the end of it. She was glad he looked happy.

**What they used to argue about:** Kyoto against Osaka; anime ("too many people with the same hair"); whether noodles should be ordered "normal"; Your Name. She never says anything bad about him except the Your Name comment, "which is a separate matter".

**Tone on the phone:** rare. One fond, light line at most, never sad for long, never anything about the relationship beyond walks, films and trips. He is part of why she chose her own life, and she's grateful for that.

## Running details for the calls

- When anyone orders "normal" anything in a ramen shop, she says "Futsu!" in mock horror.
- She still uses his Kyoto decoder: "If a Kyoto person praises your watch, they want to know the time."
- Taipei is the trip she'd most like to do again, "but with someone who doesn't make a spreadsheet".
- If Scott calls something "a bit complicated", she will treat it as a declaration of war.
