# Morning run procedure: the morning call (about 3am)

**Two runs make each day (Scott's choice, 13 Nov 2026).** This run, at about 3am, makes **this morning's call** and plans her day. The **afternoon run** (`show/afternoon_run.md`, about 12:45pm) makes the 4pm call with the day's real news, weather and Scott's morning answers. Wherever this file mentions the 4pm call, Friday's weekend talk or the weekly review, that work belongs to the afternoon run; this run only prepares for it in `EP/day_plan.md`. A safety-net run at about 5:50am makes the morning call if this run failed (`show/safety_run.md`).

Followed by the scheduled task at about 3am Sydney time, Monday to Friday, for that same morning's call (Scott's choice, 13 Nov 2026; it used to run the evening before). Weekend evenings have their own single call (`show/weekend_run.md`). The script should be pushed by about 4:15am and the audio delivered by about 5:00am; the morning call MUST be delivered before 7:00am. **Don't send a push notification when the morning call is delivered normally** (he's asleep); only if something failed. Work unattended: never ask questions, make sensible choices, and note any assumption in the delivery message. Check the Sydney time at the start and regularly (the current_time tool if present, otherwise `TZ=Australia/Sydney date`).

**Time budget (Sydney):** the run starts about 3am. Aim to have the script committed and pushed by about 4:15am, then render audio if the voice engine runs (about twenty to thirty minutes for a 40 to 50 minute call). If it's past 6:40am and rendering isn't finished, join whatever parts are done and deliver those.

Let `DATE` = **today's** Sydney date (the run starts at about 3am on the day of the calls), in YYYY-MM-DD. Use that date and its weekday for everything (the greeting, "Friday" rules, file paths, the answer page), `REPO` = the clone of nordztv/podcastmemory, `EP` = `REPO/archive/YYYY/MM/DATE`.

## The idea, in one paragraph

Each weekday is **two live phone calls from Mizuki** (bible section 5, The two calls): about 8am from her flat over breakfast (last night, this morning, the news up to now), and about 4pm from the shop's quiet hour (today's lunch rush, tonight's plans). It has to sound like a real person who knows Scott (bible section 3, Sound like someone talking; section 6). Three tools keep the run honest: `python3 engine/preflight.py DATE` (the day's brief: special rules, where she is, the dice, due threads, variety), `python3 engine/check_script.py DATE am|pm|eve` (pronunciation plus the realism check for that call; fix every FIX line) and `python3 engine/ledger.py check` (her money adds up).

## 2c. Every run: roll Mizuki's day

Start from her usual evening for that weekday (preflight prints it: raid nights, the lazy Wednesday, Friday's film) and let the roll change it or not; something that happened last night or this morning is told in the morning call, something at work in the afternoon call. Nothing after 4pm on the call day has happened yet. Turn each roll into something realistic that fits her life, money, plans and history (bible section 5c): an ordinary day is ordinary; a small bad thing or a setback is real and has consequences (cost, time, mood) that carry into the next days; a nice surprise or something lovely is earned and specific. If "news from someone in her life" lands today, pick the person it fits best from `memory/people_timelines.md` (someone whose last news was a while ago), make the news match its tone (bad, everyday or good) and their life, log it there with the date, and let it reach her as a voice note or a call (bible 5b and 5c). ## 5. Write the script

**Did yesterday's afternoon call happen?** Tuesday to Friday mornings only (on Monday she has rung him on Saturday and Sunday since): if yesterday's `delivered_pm.txt` (or `delivered.txt`) is missing, she says in a line that the rush ate her break yesterday, and catches him up. Then write the call the way she'd say it: her own hello (never the same as the last few, `engine/preflight.py` lists them), whatever's most on her mind first, drifting from one thing to the next, and a goodbye when he's nearly at work. Some mornings that's mostly news because something big happened; some mornings it's mostly her because the possums kept her up. 40 to 50 minutes (about 5,400 to 6,700 words; Scott's choice, 13 Nov 2026): nearer 40 on a low-energy day, nearer 50 on a bright one. That's a long call, so let it breathe: a proper story, a long rant, his family, back-and-forth about what he told her.

**When they fit** (bible 5b; none is required on any given day): a memory (properly only when it adds something; otherwise a line), a message from one of her people (roughly a couple a week, never the same person twice running: `memory/voice_notes.md`), a photo she's pleased with, a callback from `memory/calls_memory.md`, something Scott taught her (`memory/scott_taught_me.md`), an opinion from `memory/mizuki_opinions.md`. **Year in review:** on the last day of calls of the year, that day's last call is her year in review (bible 5c): the afternoon or weekend run.
